# Glossary of Terms (Alphabetized) 

**Anomaly**
* An entity or construct whose causal behavior cannot yet be resolved through World mediation and has not been Integrated or Indexed.
* An Anomaly remains unresolved until Causal Resolution occurs through Integration, Indexing, or the exercise of Choice.

**Blur**
* An expression of the World recognizing the Body as a causal being capable of action.
* Blur resolves a committed action as total success or total failure based on Theoretical Possibility and the Body’s Endurance.

**Body**
* The physical being originating from a reality lacking a Magical Medium.
* The Body persists across Worlds with its fundamental structure intact and does not generate or contain any internal Magical Medium, rendering it Magically Inert.

**Causal Reference**
* The manner in which the World resolves outcomes with an entity or around it, and the persistence of that reference over time.
* A stabilized Causal Reference prevents overwrite without Permission.

**Causal Resolution**
* The process by which the World resolves an Anomaly’s relationship to its causal structure.
* Causal Resolution operates independently of chronology and may take the form of Integration, Indexing, or other outcomes permitted by Recognition.

**Causal Standing**
* A finite pool accumulated by an Indexed Body through good-faith participation in World-mediated contracts and resolutions.
* Each unit of Causal Standing may be expended to invoke a single temporal rewind.

**Choice**
* The sole criterion by which the World determines whether an Anomalous being with Coherent Self-Identity is resolved with or resolved around.
* Choice requires a committed position; refusal, including rejection of a proposed framing, constitutes Choice, while conditional acceptance, deferred commitment, or absence of response does not.
* Choice is not continuously required while Indexed; it is exercised only when the World demands Choice as part of a resolution.
* If Choice is demanded during a resolution and is not exercised, Forced Assimilation occurs.

**Coherent Self-Identity** 
* The continued operation of higher cognitive functions sufficient to maintain a stable internal self-model across time, regardless of conscious awareness.
* Coherent Self-Identity is biological rather than metaphysical, independent of species and Magical Mediation, and can degrade or cease through neurological failure or death.

**Collapse**   
* The failure state in which enforced Magical Inertness ceases and the Magical Medium becomes present.
* Collapse is acutely hazardous, renders all Higher Expressions inaccessible (except Path Alignment), and exposes the Body to lethal Incompatibility unless normal function is restored.

**Compatibility**
* The presence of a sufficient substrate through which a process or effect could resolve.
Compatibility is domain-specific and does not negate Incompatibility.
 
**Contract** 
* A Permission-based agreement granting the World limited authority to resolve effects that would otherwise overwrite the Body’s state.
* Contracts do not grant abilities; they authorize scoped causal access.

**Convergence** 
* An emergency World-executed resolution state invoked when a Contract-governed resolution cannot be stabilized through normal means, using the Indexed Body as the execution vector.
* Convergence is an escalation performed by the World, not a power possessed by the Body, and preserves Choice only to the extent strictly required for execution.

**Conviction**
* The willingness to accept the consequences of fully committing to an action or outcome.
* Conviction is required for Path and Blur and represents acceptance rather than confidence or certainty.

**Deferral (Terminal Resolution Deferral)**
* A World-level resolution state in which otherwise lethal physical outcomes are delayed while the entity remains a coherent causal participant.
* Underlying conditions remain unchanged but are withheld from physical manifestation.
* When Deferral ends, all withheld conditions resume ordinary physical progression according to their true physical state.

**Dominion**
* When Indexed, the Body’s Magical Inertness exists as a field rather than a point.  
* That field is Dominion.

**Endurance** 
* The Body’s capacity to sustain uninterrupted physical exertion.
* Blur succeeds only if the resolved exertion can be maintained to completion; injury and pain are irrelevant unless they terminate exertion.

**Feedback**
* A Recognition channel permitted by the World when mediated perception fails and Integration is resisted, enabling reciprocal awareness sufficient to preserve Choice.
* Feedback manifests as non-verbal awareness when Intent involving the Body intersects, without conveying interpretation, motive, or propositional information.

**Forced Assimilation**  
* The higher-order form of Integration used for Anomalies possessing the additional resistance associated with Coherent Self-Identity.
* Mechanically identical to Integration; distinguished only by the additional resistance being overcome, not by punishment or hostility.

**Higher Expressions**
* Recognition-based manifestations arising from Mutual Recognition and the process of achieving Indexing, rather than from magical capacity. Their function is to establish and preserve the conditions under which the Body can remain Indexed.
* Higher Expressions are Path, Blur, Dominion, Feedback, Jinx, and Contract-mediated enforcement states.

**Indexing**  
* The process by which the Body becomes a stabilized causal being with respect to the World following Mutual Recognition.
* Indexing is not a Contract and does not require Choice.

**Indexed Partnership** 
* A Contract-authorized state in which the World permits two Indexed entities to be resolved as a single causal system for a specific resolution, while preserving separate Coherent Self-Identity and non-delegable Choice.

**Indexed State**  
* A condition in which the Body is a stabilized Causal Reference within the World.
* Actions persist, and overwrite of state requires explicit Permission or higher-order intervention.

**Incompatibility**
* The absence of a Compatible Magical Medium between a magical process and its target, preventing that process from binding, resolving, or persisting.
* Incompatibility is not resistance, suppression, or negation; the process cannot be realized because the required medium is absent at the point of interaction.

**Integration**
* A form of Causal Resolution in which the World proceeds without Mutual Recognition.
* Integration preserves physical persistence but not external identity and does not enable Higher Expressions.

**Intent**  
* A consciously held desired outcome or state toward which the user is prepared to act.
* Intent defines direction but does not enforce causality or guarantee success.

**Intrinsic State**
* The fullest expression of the non-sentient causal construct representing a Body, defined outside time and causality and consistent with its irreversible structure and history, absent contingent suppression or external constraint.
* Evaluation of Choice, when required, occurs relative to this state.

**Jinx**
* Residual Incompatibility imposed on magical systems forced to resolve without access to the Magical Medium.
* Jinx degrades the reliability of activating magical processes, without targeting, Intent, or direct causation.

**Magic**
* Any effect whose occurrence or operation lies outside the theoretical possibility of our known reality (not the setting reality).

**Magical Mediation**
* The continued involvement of the Magical Medium in sustaining or enforcing an effect.
* If removal of mediation causes a phenomenon to cease or change category, it is not considered physical.

**Magical Medium**
* The underlying substrate through which Magic binds, resolves, persists, or enforces causality.
* The Body lacks Compatibility with this medium entirely; Magic cannot apply where no medium exists.

**Magically Inert**
* A property describing the absence of any internal Magical Medium, preventing participation in Magical Mediation.
* Inert matter cannot couple to, sustain, or transmit non-physical magical forces, while remaining fully subject to ordinary physical processes.

**Mutual Recognition** 
* A state in which the Body and the World are mutually recognized as stabilized causal structures.
* It arises only when Integration fails due to the Body’s Coherent Self-Identity and is required for Indexing.

**Path**
* An expression of the Body recognizing the World as it may be coherently traversed.
* Path reveals whether a Viable Path exists toward an intended outcome the user is capable of enacting.
* Where multiple routes exist, Path reflects differences in total energy expenditure, not probability, moral value, or risk.

**Permission**
* Explicit consent given by the user allowing the World to overwrite, bind, rewind, or enforce causality upon the Body.
* Permission will never override present refusal.

**Physical Phenomenon**
* A state, force, or process that persists and operates solely through local physical law once initiated.
* A phenomenon is considered physical only if it no longer requires ongoing Magical Mediation to exist or function.

**Physical Possibility**
* A condition in which an action could be completed under ideal physical conditions using only the Body and what is recognized as part of the user at initiation.
* It considers structural, mechanical, and environmental constraints, but excludes Endurance as a limiting factor.

**Recognition**
* The acknowledgment of a being as a coherent, stable participant in causality.
* Recognition may be one-way or mutual and determines how the World resolves interactions involving the Body.

**Snapback**
* The immediate physiological reconciliation that occurs when Blur fails after resolving a complete, physically valid trajectory. 
* No displacement or partial execution occurs.

**Structural Failure**
* Damage to the Body that affects its ability to continue exertion.
* Structural Failure counts as Endurance failure only if it would prevent continued exertion under ideal conditions.

**Terminal Resolution**
* The final closure of a being’s participation in the World’s causal processes, after which no further correction, re-evaluation, delay, or continuation occurs.
* Terminal Resolution may occur through death or other irreversible loss of causal coherence.

**Viable Path**
* A coherent sequence of actions, events, and conditions that could lead to an intended outcome given the user’s capabilities, constraints, and Conviction.
* It need not be safe, fast, or likely, only possible.
 
**World**
* The total causal system within which all entities, structures, and processes are resolved.
* The World is not an agent, will, or consciousness, but the complete framework of causal evaluation and enforcement.
* References to the World acting, preferring, or conducting resolution describe invariant systemic behavior rather than intention.

**World-Mediated Recognition**
* A Recognition state in which the World permits an Indexed causal construct and a selected Integrated being to be resolved as a single coherent causal system for a specific resolution, without transferring agency, authority, or Choice between them.



## Non-Glossary Terms

Not glossary but these terms are not interchangeable. If they come across that way, please point out the mistake. 

1. **Causal handling** (World) — the grouping and treatment of entities during Causal Resolution.
2. **Engagement** (Body) — interfacing with an already-present recognitional process.
    - Engagement does not activate, deactivate, grant, or withdraw Path, Blur, Dominion, or Feedback.
    - All such expressions are constantly present while Indexed and produce perceptible or consequential output only when their engagement conditions are met.
3. **Progress** (all) — movement of a process toward completion (If it could be plotted on a timeline without Choice or permission, progress is valid)
4. **Overwrite** (World) — any action by the World that replaces, rewinds, binds, or enforces a state upon the Body without its participation or Permission.
5. **Fail/Failure** (all) — non-realization of an attempted resolution or action.
6. **Exposure** (all) — contact with a medium or process without mediation or insulation. Exposure describes a condition of contact, not a consequence. Exposure alone does not imply damage, harm, Collapse, or outcome; such effects depend on Incompatibility, duration, and governing constraints.
7. **Pressure** (body): Perceptual indication of constraint or alignment, not force, coercion, or obligation.
8. **Alignment** (body) — the degree of Compatibility between the Body’s current state and an engaged viable continuation.
9. **Capability** (body) — what the Body can physically and causally do under ideal conditions, independent of permission, recognition, or outcome. 

### World (Causal Authority) vs Body (Embodied Agency) Terms

The World operates only at the level of causal resolution. It cannot directly apply effect-level Magic to the Body. Effect-level magical phenomena may only be realized when Dominion is voluntarily reduced and only through ordinary mediation by external agents or systems.
 
1. Resolved vs Realized
    - **Resolved** (World) — Resolution-level causal determination.
    - **Realized** (Body) — effect-level manifestation or experience.
2. Proceed vs Continuation and Continue
    - **Proceed** (World) — whether the World allows a process to move forward at all.
    - **Continuation** (Body) — the existence of a viable causal extension. This refers to whether further action is possible at all, regardless of enactment.
    - **Continue** (Body) — the ongoing enactment of an action or state without interruption. This refers to enactment, not viability.
3. Resolution vs Outcome
    - **Resolution** (World) — closure or stabilization of causality.
    - **Outcome** (Body) — a realized state or condition that may be intended, failed, deferred, or realized, and does not require prior Resolution.
      - Outcomes may be realized during unresolved, unstable, or deferred states and do not imply causal closure.
4. Participation vs Persistence
    - **Participation** (World) — resolution that includes the Body as a causal reference, such that outcomes are resolved with the Body rather than around it.
    - **Persistence** (Body) — the Body continues to exist across resolution attempts, without implying protection, durability, safety, success, or optimization.
5. Stabilize vs Stable
    - **Stabilize** (World) — a procedural condition in which no further forced resolution is required. Does not imply safety, permanence, success, or protection.
    - **Stable** (Body) — a descriptive condition in which change is not rapid and coherence is temporarily maintained. Applies to Body state, Path signals, Dominion behavior, and experience.

**Short List**

* Resolved vs Realized
* Proceed vs Continuation and Continue
* Resolution vs Outcome
* Participation vs Persistence
* Stabilize vs Stable
