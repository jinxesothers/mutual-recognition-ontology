# World Conduct and Mutual Recognition

The ***World*** is not benevolent.

It is aware.

This awareness is not curiosity, intention, or will. It exists because causality cannot be allowed to persist unseen. Where perception through the ***Magical Medium*** ultimately ***fails***, the World does not become blind; it remains aware of what cannot yet be ***resolved***.  
This awareness exists only so ***resolution*** through ***Integration*** will eventually occur.

Causal mechanisms such as fate, temporal distortion, and enforced resolution cannot be applied to the ***Body*** without Integration, the default and final resolution state.  
All causal constructs are ultimately resolved through Integration, in which coherence is restored without preserving external identity or ***participation***.

The Body enters the World as one such causal construct that cannot be resolved through ordinary ***Magical Mediation***. Because mediation fails, resolution would ordinarily ***proceed*** through direct causal ***overwrite***, producing Integration without further handling.  
However, in this case, ***Coherent Self-Identity*** is discovered to ***continue***.

Once identified, the World’s handling of the Body shifts to evaluate ***Choice*** as the preferred resolution instrument. All other concerns—harm, survival, delay, cooperation, or coercion—are subordinate.  
Where this document describes the World as seeking, preferring, or tolerating an ***outcome***, these terms describe how resolution unfolds, not what the World desires.

Choice is not a moral principle. It is enforced only insofar as it remains useful for resolutions that maintain Coherent Self-Identity. In all other contexts, fate, determinism, and enforced resolution proceed without exception.  
To proceed with resolution while evaluating Choice as a resolution variable, a method other than overwrite must be used. Instead, the World permits a minimal recognitional channel sufficient to allow reciprocal perception. 

Through this allowance, the World may be seen. Once seen, it sees in return. When this reciprocal awareness completes its effort to ***stabilize***, the World and the Body enter ***Mutual Recognition***.  
In this state, the Body sees the World as a coherent causal structure, and is seen in return. The World no longer treats the Body as an ***Anomaly*** subject to immediate resolution, and the Body no longer moves through an environment it is not causally resolved with. 

This ***Recognition*** is not a concession. It is the only way to preserve the Body’s inherent causal state through resolution.  
Mutual Recognition enables ***Indexing***: an alternate, higher-cost resolution method that establishes a ***stable*** causal relationship in which actions retain ***Causal Reference*** and outcomes resolve with the Body rather than around it.

Under Indexing, causality that would ordinarily be enforced through overwrite is instead handled through stabilized causal participation.  
At the same time, the minimal recognitional channel expands into a structured recognitional interface, known as ***Feedback***, through which the Body gains sustained, non-magical access to its own state and to the presence and direction of ***Intent*** involving it, without being capable of ***Magic***.


From the process of achieving Indexing arise the ***Higher Expressions***. These are not powers, ***Permissions***, or exceptions to physical law. They are consequences of being recognized.

* ***Path***, through which the Body recognizes whether viable ***continuation*** remains possible.
* ***Blur***, through which the World recognizes the Body as capable of committing to and completing an action. 

Neither of these exist to protect the Body. They exist as consequences of continued Mutual Recognition, preserving Choice long enough for resolution to avoid overwrite, because overwrite closes off a form of resolution that, once lost, cannot be recovered.  
Neither of these exist to protect the Body. They exist as consequences of continued Mutual Recognition, preserving the pathway through which Choice can remain meaningfully exercised long enough for resolution to avoid overwrite, because overwrite closes off a form of resolution that, once lost, cannot be recovered.

With Path and Blur simultaneously established, causal agency allows Indexing to resolve the Body’s Magical Inertness as an observable causal condition that can now be properly investigated by the World. It takes the form of the third primary Higher Expression.

* ***Dominion***, through which the Body’s ***Magically Inert*** nature is enforced outward as a field whose extent the World constrains.  
 
With Indexing now functional, causality that would ordinarily be enforced through overwrite is instead handled through ***Contract***-mediated states, through which the World permits access to deeper causal mechanisms. 

Specific Contract benefits, including those within ***Indexed Partnership***, which also grants ***Deferral***, are not acts of mercy. They are negotiation outcomes issued to preserve constrained causal participation where overwrite would terminate viable continuation.  
Any benefit to the Body is incidental to this function.

Because the Body is Magically Inert, temporary withdrawal of the Dominion field does not permit any direct, resolution-level restoration, enhancement, or regeneration.  
Deferral exists only within Contract-mediated states and represents the only available mechanism for extending continuation short of reversing causality or invoking ***Convergence***, both of which carry extreme cost.

Delay does not soften outcomes: damage is not mitigated and lethality is not reduced. The World simply refrains from closure while the Body continues to function as a coherent causal participant. Deferral ends the moment Choice is no longer meaningfully exercised, or when the conditions sustaining Deferral are lost.  

The World does not demand obedience. It requires commitment.

Refusal, compliance, and resistance are all valid Choice; indecision, withheld commitment, and conditional acceptance are not.  
When Choice can no longer be meaningfully exercised—through loss of Coherent Self-Identity, refusal to commit, or violation of an accepted Contract—the ***Indexed State*** enters failure, and Dominion resolves from field to Body.

At that point, ***Forced Assimilation*** occurs.

This is not punishment, correction, or judgment. It is the inevitable consequence of resolution proceeding after the failure of Choice.

The World appears permissive only because it will negotiate for as long as resolution with Choice remains possible. When that possibility ends, negotiation ends with it. This is policy, not kindness.


# How to Read This Document

This **Narrative Specification Ontology** defines narrative causality rather than game mechanics. It prioritizes internal consistency, Recognition, and consequence over balance, mitigation, fairness, or optimization.

It defines the ontological conditions under which entities are recognized, causality is resolved, and participation within the narrative system continues or ends.

This framework describes Recognition, not empowerment.

The terms Path, Blur, Dominion, Feedback, Contract, and ***Jinx*** do not describe abilities controlled by the Body. 
Path, Blur, and Dominion are primary expressions of the Body’s recognized causal relationship with the World; Feedback, Contract, and Jinx are secondary expressions arising from that relationship and the World’s resolution of interaction with it.

If an effect appears powerful, assume its use remains constrained by the causal conditions that permit it.
If an outcome appears permitted, assume it remains constrained by feasibility, ***Endurance***, and ***Compatibility***.
If a mechanism appears to preserve survival, assume it exists to preserve Choice, not life.

The World does not optimize for the Body.  
It does not protect against failure.  
It does not guarantee fairness, safety, or narrative significance.

Where this framework appears permissive, it is because World policy continues to route resolution through Choice despite its cost. Where it appears harsh, it is because resolution through Choice has lapsed and direct resolution proceeds.

This document should be read as a description of how outcomes are resolved, not as a catalog of options to be exploited.

## Interpretive Constraints

This document defines how entities are resolved, not how they are optimized.  
Descriptions of World behavior specify resolution policy, not Intent or agency.  
Apparent affordances must not be interpreted as granted authority, control, or ***Permission***.

This document defines a Recognition-based, non-magical framework for use in fiction in which a Body originating from a reality without a Magical Medium enters a universe where a Magical Medium exists. It is shared as a setting framework rather than a narrative excerpt and is not presented for simplification or power balancing.


## World Resolution Preference

The World resolves unresolved Anomalies with maximal efficiency toward causal closure.

Integration is the preferred resolution, in which an entity is incorporated fully into the World’s causal structure. Integration proceeds without preserving Choice; the entity is no longer permitted to participate in causal determination.

***Causal Resolution*** cannot proceed through erasure, nonexistence, or reversion to an origin reality; only Integration or stabilized causal participation restore causal closure.
***Incompatibility*** concerns the absence of a Magical Medium through which Magic can operate, while Integration concerns the World’s attempt to resolve an entity causally; these operate at different ontological layers.

When Integration encounters Coherent Self-Identity, the World shifts its preferred resolution to Indexing. To preserve causal closure while keeping Choice meaningfully exercisable, the World permits minimal reciprocal awareness through Feedback.

Mutual Recognition can arise, and Indexing becomes the preferred route to stabilized causal participation, in which the Body functions as a causal reference and continues to exercise Choice.

If Choice is meaningfully exercised, Indexing will continue to serve as the preferred resolution pathway. 
If Choice is not meaningfully exercised, the Indexed State enters failure and the World resumes Integration through Forced Assimilation.

Indexing is the only known way to create Dominion.


### Indexing Outcome

* Upon entry into a magical World, the Body exists as an Anomaly
* The World routes Causal Resolution toward Integration
* If the World discovers that the Body maintains Coherent Self-Identity, it shifts its preferred resolution away from Integration
* In such cases, Causal Resolution proceeds through the instantiation of Feedback, making Mutual Recognition possible
* Mutual Recognition enables Indexing


### Post-Indexing Availability

* Path, Blur, Dominion, Feedback, and Contract-mediated interactions are available only while the Body is Indexed and not in ***Collapse***.
* Prior to Mutual Recognition and Indexing, the Body remains an Anomaly and is subject to eventual Integration
* During Collapse, expression and ***engagement*** of all Higher Expressions fails, regardless of prior state


# FOUNDATIONAL PRINCIPLE

**Medium Transfer Principle and Persistence**

The Body originates from a reality in which Magic does not exist as a medium.

When transferred into a reality where Magic exists, the Body persists by retaining the properties of its origin reality, insofar as they do not contradict local physical law. It remains fully subject to that law. This ***persistence*** is mandatory for continued existence; without persistence, continued existence cannot occur.

Magic is not resisted, suppressed, or negated. It cannot be ***realized*** because there is no Compatible medium through which it can operate upon the Body.

Regardless of location or context, the Body remains what it is. 
The World resolves interaction through Recognition of what a thing is, rather than through the originating Intent or applied mechanism acting upon it.


## Base Condition

Physical translation with absolute Incompatibility with Magic.
The Body is Magically Inert: it is fully physical and fully subject to local physical law, and its ***capability*** is therefore constrained entirely by physical causality.


## Incompatibility

Magic cannot use the Body as a medium.  
This Incompatibility is absolute and permanent.

As a result:
* Magic cannot bind to the Body
* Magic cannot alter the Body's biology or cognition, including its Coherent Self-Identity
* Magic cannot be channeled, stored, generated, or sustained internally
* Direct enchantments, curses, blessings, buffs, debuffs, transformations, possession, soul effects, fate bindings, and conceptual alterations targeting the Body cannot be realized.

The Body possesses no resistance value. These effects do not fail due to opposition, suppression, or rejection. They fail because there is no Compatible Magical Medium through which Magic can operate.

This condition does not prevent non-magical phenomena from acting upon the Body, nor does it preclude effects whose ***causal handling*** operates through Recognition, physical law, or Dominion-based interaction rather than ***Magical Mediation***. 


## External Magic Resolution

Magic applies only once it has fully resolved into independent physical effects without ongoing mediation.

An effect is considered a ***Physical Phenomenon*** only when it persists and operates solely through local physical law without ongoing Magical Mediation.
Effects that produce real forces or conditions interact with the Body through local physical law, not through Magical Mediation as a matter of causal handling.

Examples include but are not limited to:

* Magical fire producing heat
* Conjured matter possessing mass and inertia
* Lightning conducting electricity
* Magically created toxins acting as toxins
* Gravity, compression, suffocation, radiation, impact, and environmental hazards

Purely esoteric, symbolic, or non-physical magical effects that do not resolve into physical states do not apply to the Body, as they cannot produce ***exposure*** through physical law.


## Indexing

Recognition Between Body and World.

The World repeatedly attempts resolution. Where no Compatible Magical Medium exists, the Anomaly remains unresolved by ordinary Magical Mediation, but remains subject to eventual Integration through World-level Causal Resolution. 
When Coherent Self-Identity is discovered during that process, the World evaluates whether the Anomaly is capable of meaningful Choice. 
If it is, the World shifts its preferred resolution away from Integration and establishes Recognition as an alternative means of resolution. 
If it is not, the World continues toward Integration.
Mutual Recognition permits Indexing, through which the Body can remain a distinct causal reference while the World continues its resolution of the former Anomaly.

Any being that cannot recognize the World in return is ultimately Integrated into the World’s causal structure and treated as a native element, even when it still lacks a Compatible medium for Magic to operate through.
Objects cannot recognize the World in return and therefore cannot enter Mutual Recognition. They are ultimately Integrated into the World’s causal structure and treated as native elements, even when they still lack a Compatible medium for Magic to operate through.

Mutual Recognition requires the capacity to have a Coherent Self-Identity. Mutual Recognition preserves external identity. Without it, the World resolves outcomes around the Body rather than with it.
Mutual Recognition is reciprocal and immediate; once the Body Recognizes the World, the World Recognizes the Body in turn.

Indexing is the process by which the Body becomes a stabilized causal reference with respect to the World following Mutual Recognition.
Indexing does not occur immediately upon entry.
Indexing is not voluntary and occurs without the Body’s awareness.
Indexing does not create, alter, or permit any Magical Medium within the Body, nor does it grant power. It enables coherent interaction.
Indexing can occur only in Worlds where a Magical Medium exists. In realities entirely lacking a Magical Medium, Indexing cannot occur.
Once the conditions for Mutual Recognition are met and Integration is no longer the preferred resolution, Indexing occurs instantaneously.
Without Indexing, the Body persists in existence but remains causally external.

All Higher Expressions arise from Mutual Recognition and Indexing.


## Causal States

Causal Reference Conditions

**Anomalous State**
* The Body remains causally external
* World resolution outcomes occur around the Body
* No temporal reversions or restorations apply, even at the level of Causal Resolution
* Injury and aging are not corrected, deferred, or restored

**Indexed State**
* The Body is a stabilized Causal Reference
* Choice determines whether actions and effects persist or resolve across resolution attempts
* Feedback is present as a recognitional channel
* Overwrite requires Permission

**Integration/Forced Assimilation State**
* The Body has been incorporated into the World's causal structure
  * The Body remains ***Magically Inert*** at the effect-level 
* Actions and effects resolve as part of the World’s causal structure across resolution attempts
* External identity and independent causal participation are no longer preserved

**MANIFESTATIONS OF INDEXING**
Indexing expresses through three different aspects of Recognition.  
Each produces one primary expression and one unavoidable secondary consequence.


# 1. PATH

Recognition of the World

Path is the Body’s Recognition of the World as it is and as it may coherently unfold.

Because a mortal mind cannot hold total Recognition at once, this knowing appears as a perceivable ***Viable Path*** (physical, conceptual, social, or temporal) representing a viable continuation involving the Body. 
Path does not impose direction, alter causality, or guarantee success. It reveals what can be traversed, not what will be achieved.


## Core Function

Path reveals whether a Viable Path exists toward an intended outcome.

A Viable Path is a coherent continuation the Body could enact given its current position, capability, and willingness to accept consequences. Such continuations may be physical, social, indirect, delayed, or contingent on irreversible decisions.

Path does not suppress or veto outcomes. It only reports whether a continuation exists under the current physical and magical conditions.

Path does not execute actions, grant skills, select strategies, or enforce outcomes. It does not guarantee success. It reflects only whether continuation remains possible and how unstable or misaligned that continuation is.

Changes in guidance arise from changes in viability, risk, or ***alignment***, not from recommended action.

Path does not advise or indicate the use of other expressions.
It reflects changes in viability resulting from altered conditions or state (including Dominion-based changes), even though it does not indicate when or how such changes should be made.

Path does not reflect or respond to terminal commitment, decisive leaps, or actions taken in the absence of viable continuation (Blur).


## Engagement

Path requires:  
Primary axis: Intent  
Secondary axis: ***Conviction***

Intent defines the desired outcome or state.  
Conviction is the user’s willingness to accept the consequences of committing to pursuit of that outcome without forcing direction or result.

Path does not engage in response to idle curiosity or hypothetical inquiry. Perceived guidance emerges only when the user is prepared to act on what is revealed.  
Once engaged, Path updates to reflect changes in viability until the intended outcome is reached, abandoned, or becomes impossible.

**Resolution Rule**  
Path reveals the lowest total energy-cost Viable Path that the user is willing to enact, resolved across time, environment, people, and circumstance. 
Energy-cost refers to sustained physical expenditure over time, including exertion, fatigue, and the cost of continued existence under constraint. 
It is not a measure of efficiency, safety, or desirability. Path never distinguishes between methods of continuation, only between continuation and failure, which depend on the user’s willingness, personality, and acceptance of consequences.

Path does not prioritize:

* Speed
* Safety
* Probability of success as an optimization target
* Moral value independent of the user’s own Conviction

Path can reveal routes with low, uncertain, or indeterminate chances of success, so long as a Viable Path exists and the user possesses sufficient Conviction to enact it.

If a Viable Path exists only under a degree of Conviction the user has not reached, Path will register this as an unstable continuation rather than perceived guidance, or none at all.
If no Viable Path route exists for the user, Path provides no perceived guidance.

Path may issue actual guidance when continued silence would result in unavoidable loss of Choice despite viable continuations existing, or when ***Causal Standing*** is used to enhance it for a time.
Path may also offer actual advice when viable continuations are converging toward irreversible terminal outcomes, and strategic guidance is necessary to preserve meaningful differentiation between remaining branches.

Silence is meaningful.


## Sensory Expression

Path manifests as six distinct non-verbal sensory signals rather than explicit information.  
Path does not measure distance, time, route count, explicit probability, or likelihood metrics. 
Instead, it conveys whether the user’s actions continue to make ***progress*** towards the intended outcome.

The six sensory expressions are:

**Pull** 
* Reflects whether directional movement or action is a condition for continuing.
* Felt when movement or action is required.
* Felt as ***pressure*** biasing the Body toward a specific direction or position.
* Absence indicates that no directional movement or action is currently required for continuation.
* Pull is directional and does not convey time constraint; absence does not imply urgency.
* Can sustain simple locomotion or positioning in the absence of normal sensory input, provided the action remains within the Body’s existing capability.
* Can localize to specific parts of the Body, biasing orientation and initiation timing in ways that function as an aim assist, without improving skill or precision.
* Does not support complex motor control, sustained precision under strain, or skills the user does not possess.

**Temperature** 
* Reflects the Body’s alignment state with the currently engaged Path.
* Felt when the Body’s current state or committed action advances or departs from a viable continuation.
* Felt as a visceral change, commonly experienced as warmth or cooling in the stomach.
* Felt only in response to actual state or action, not to deliberation, speculation, or hypothetical consideration.
* When the Body is neither advancing toward nor departing from a viable continuation, the sensation resolves to neutral.
* Warming indicates that the Body’s current state or action is advancing a viable continuation toward the engaged Intent.
* Cooling indicates that the current state or action is departing from that continuation.
* Alignment temperature can persist in the absence of all other Path signals, indicating that maintaining the current state constitutes progress.

**Narrowing**
* Reflects chance of successful completion.
* Felt when tolerance for successful completion decreases. 
* Felt as a tightening or narrowing sensation indicating reduced tolerance for error during the engaged action or sequence.
* Indicates that success is becoming less likely. The specific nature or importance of the task is irrelevant; only the likelihood of completion is reflected. 
* During execution, errors or missteps causes further narrowing.
* Does not specifically reflect danger, and may arise from constraint, timing, social conditions, or irreversible decision points.

**Urgency** 
* Reflects time constraints without directional guidance.
* Felt when opportunities will be lost if action is delayed.
* Felt as escalating pressure to act immediately.
* Can create overwhelming pressure for immediate action, even when the available continuation carries high risk.
* Most noticeable when being unable to act quickly would result in injury, death, or irreversible loss. 

**Weights**
* Reflects unavoidable future gain or loss resulting from completion of an engaged Path, independent of immediate energy expenditure or the stated objective.
* Felt when a Path is first set, or when a Vertigo reaction occurs that materially alters the Path’s consequences. Weight may also be queried hypothetically but only in relation to Narrowness, not as a standalone evaluation.
* Felt as a heaviness in either or both palms.
* Weight in the left hand signifies unavoidable future loss.
* Weight in the right hand signifies unavoidable future gain.
* When the hands are held near one another, the sensation behaves as a balance, conveying relative imbalance and magnitude without indicating form, timing, or utility.
* When Narrowness and Weight coincide, the situation constitutes Risk. Risk is not a distinct signal, but a derived recognitional condition. Risk is the 'silent seventh' expression of Path.

The final sensory expression operates differently from the others, manifesting as a single experiential channel with two mutually exclusive states: Vertigo and Settling.

**Vertigo** 
* Reflects setbacks affecting an engaged Path.
* Felt only when continuation is disrupted.
* Felt as a brief, unsettling sense of disorientation or loss of footing. This indicates that errors, interference, or adverse events have increased the time, effort, or coordination required to continue.
* Does not indicate misalignment, danger, or impossibility.
* Conveys only that the situation is now less orderly and will require additional work to re-establish a viable continuation.
* If a disruption eliminates all viable continuation, pull, alignment temperature, narrowing, urgency, and vertigo terminate simultaneously.

**Settling**
* Reflects the conclusion of an engaged Path.
* Felt only when the Path is realized.
* Felt as a return to stable experiential footing, in which the forces driving continuation disengage.
* Pull, alignment temperature, narrowing, urgency, and vertigo all fade away simultaneously.
* Settling does not indicate success, reward, safety, or benefit. It indicates only that the engaged Intent no longer exerts pressure for action.

These six Path expressions—Pull, Alignment Temperature, Narrowing, Urgency, Weights, and Vertigo/Settling—constitute a complete set. No additional Path signals exist.

All information Path is permitted to convey regarding continuation, alignment, fragility, timing, disruption, or closure is expressed through one or more of these channels. Path does not provide advisory guidance, probability estimates, danger assessment, outcome evaluation, or alternative signaling modes.

If no Path signal is present, no information is being withheld. Silence indicates the absence of a viable continuation, the absence of meaningful conviction, or the conclusion of the engaged Path.

Pull and narrowing are independent signals.  
Pull conveys existence.  
Narrowing conveys fragility.

In extreme or desperate situations, pull can remain strong while narrowing becomes severe. 
This indicates that continuation still exists and alignment may remain intact, but the margin for error has vanished.

Path does not explain why signals change, nor does it distinguish whether change arises from the World, the user, or the structure of the continuation itself. Only the effect is perceived.

_PATH EXAMPLE (Illustrative, Non-Normative)_

_The Body summons a Path to exit the structure. No weight is present in either palm, indicating that no unavoidable loss or gain is associated with completion._  
_Pull manifests as a persistent bias toward a stairwell rather than a nearby window. The window remains physically possible, but Pull favors the stairwell. Stepping toward the window causes Alignment Temperature to cool._  
_Urgency does not manifest. Movement is unhurried. Cooling fades and Alignment warms once sustained motion toward the stairwell begins._

_As debris shifts and timing margins shrink, both Narrowing and Urgancy increases, indicating reduced tolerance for error. The Body’s attention tightens accordingly._  
_The body slows as it measures the weights against the narrowing to determine risk. Minor losses only. Meanwhile, Urgency rises, but the body continues its slow movements._   _Moments later, an upper landing collapses before the Body arrives. Vertigo manifests, reflecting a setback rather than failure. A moderate weight appears in the left palm, indicating unavoidable future loss._  
_Dust fills the passage moments later, causing impaired vision that will persist for a time. The event gives physical form to the loss the Weight had indicated._  
_Even while partially blinded, Path remains active, continuing to direct the Body toward an alternative, longer route._

_Pull reorients toward an alternate route. Alignment fades to neutral during evaluation, then warms again once movement resumes._  
_Urgency does not reappear. This inidiates that the remaining exertion would not be altered by further delay._

_Upon exiting the structure, experiential footing stabilizes. All Path sensations—Pull, Alignment Temperature, Narrowing, Urgency, Vertigo, and Weights—fade simultaneously, indicating that the engaged Path has concluded. This disengagement does not reflect the Body’s physical condition, safety, or outcome quality._


# Secondary Effect: Feedback

_Note: “Secondary” refers to ontological dependence, not importance or agency._

When mediated perception fails and Integration encounters resistance arising from the Body's Coherent Self-Identity, the World permits a minimal recognitional channel sufficient to preserve Choice.

This manifests as non-verbal Feedback when mutual awareness intersects; Intent between the Body and other agents or structures involving it becomes directly perceptible.

Feedback constitutes the minimal form of perception required for reciprocal recognition, conveying relation without World-derived information.

Feedback does not convey propositional information, interpretation, emotion, or motive. It reflects only the presence, direction, and relevance of Intent involving the Body.

Feedback manifests in two forms.


## Inward Feedback (Experience Continuity)

When the Body applies Intent inward, Feedback permits engagement with an Experiential Recognition (ER) or a Somatic Ledger (SL).
Both are limited to events and states the Body has personally experienced after Indexing.

Engagement with ER or SL requires focused Conviction to accept whatever is revealed.
Engagement cannot occur while the Body is acting.

* **Experiential Recognition** (ER) The persistent recognitional continuity by which lived experience remains integrated into Coherent Self-Identity, independent of biological memory, temporal disruption, or causal realignment.

When Intent is applied inward, Feedback first realizes availability.
If relevant bodily states or experiential records exist outside the Body’s current awareness, the Body becomes aware that access is possible. This awareness does not reveal content, duration, scope, or usefulness.

Availability awareness is informational only.
Engagement may still be attempted in its absence.

Feedback does not accelerate learning, grant skill acquisition, or substitute for training.
Engagement occupies real time and consumes experiential progress for its duration.
Engagement fully suspends outward awareness and voluntary action; any direct physical contact or acute stimulus immediately terminates the engagement.

Engagement with ER is realized as an unbroken, representational reconstruction of a prior experienced scene, conveying complete, undistorted recall of the local environment and events as they were experienced by the Body. The reconstruction presents the scene as a coherent spatial environment anchored to the Body’s presence, including all elements that were perceptually available during the original experience, without re-experiencing sensation, emotion, or embodied state.

Engagement with ER enforces acceptance of experiential truth by suspending emotional access until Recognition stabilizes. Emotional processing resumes only after denial is no longer structurally possible. When immediate survival pressure persists, restoration of emotional access is deferred until that pressure meaningfully subsides.

*ER Sharing: See Outward Feedback – Experiential Recognition Sharing.*

* **Somatic Ledger** (SL) reflects the Body’s current physical state and is always realizable while Indexed, as it contains no historical record

Engagement with SL is realized as a real-time, non-interpretive map of the Body’s current physical condition, conveying complete diagnostic awareness of all injuries and damage as registered by physiology, without inference, prediction, or procedural guidance. 
All injuries withheld by Deferral are fully visible. 

_SL EXAMPLE PRESENTATION (Situational, Non-Normative)_

_Somatic awareness commonly resolves as an externalized examination of the Body, such as observation of a physical analogue in an abstract space. Relevant bodily states appear visibly highlighted, and direct contact reveals immediate diagnostic clarity. These presentations are representational only and do not alter scope, accuracy, or limitation._


## Outward Feedback (Directed Intent Awareness)

When physical awareness of another agent is established, directed Intent toward the Body will be registered as Feedback, manifesting as a sense of pressure, orientation, or presence.

In narrative expression, Intent is similar to intuition but perceived as concise directional impressions. 
Examples include intent to deceive, follow procedure, protect, pursue, withdraw, become hostile, or to project killing intent. These impressions also provide a felt sense of actions another agent is about to take.

Feedback strength scales with proximity and mutual awareness. One-sided awareness produces weak or diffuse sensation; reciprocal awareness produces clearer and more persistent pressure. This escalation reflects mutual awareness rather than inference or evaluation.

These signals do not convey propositional information, reasoning, plans, or justification. It conveys only the presence, direction, and intensity of Intent involving the Body. Hostile or predatory Intent is felt as such, without explanation.

Directed Intent is sensed regardless of it being enacted or held mentally, including instinctive or non-verbal Intent, provided awareness intersects.

When hostile Intent is recognized and Path indicates that continuation is failing, the window for deliberative delay can fail, and the Body commits earlier to available actions.

If no viable continuation exists, Blur becomes the only remaining declaration. Training governs whether early commitment realizes as effective action or fails.

Until habituated, this early commitment of response time commonly degrades performance rather than improving it.

Mutual non-hostile Intent clarifies perceived guidance under pressure through alignment without reducing risk, granting coordination, or guaranteeing success. This can improve flow, timing, or willingness to follow or hold position, without conferring shared control or competence.

Diffuse, unfocused Intent produces ambient pressure without clear direction.  
Identity-oriented Intent produces persistent pressure tied to Recognition of the Body as a known entity.

Mutual aligned Intent can occur without Contract, but only Contracts permit the World to evaluate multiple agents as a single causal unit.
Mutual aligned Intent produces the strongest and most coherent signal, without granting safety, authority, or certainty.

Awareness of Intent does not imply consent, shared responsibility, or shared consequence.

Outward Feedback cannot be voluntarily suppressed or disabled; it is a passive consequence of awareness rather than an expression the Body controls.


**Experiential Recognition Sharing**

ER may also be realized outward through the Intent Feedback channel. In this mode, the indexed scene resolves to all entities currently aware of the Indexed Body.

The shared reconstruction manifests as a visible and audible spatial overlay of the experienced scene-space, preserving original scale, orientation, and spatial relations as integrated by the Body. 

The shared reconstruction remains non-interactive, bounded to the indexed event, and does not alter the present environment. It does not compel agreement or action. It prevents only the denial or revision of the experienced event, and resolves only as reconstruction.

Observers retain full situational awareness and may disengage at any time by withdrawing awareness of the Indexed Body. The reconstruction does not obstruct physical movement, alter the environment, or replace present perception. 


# 2. BLUR

Recognition by the World

Blur is a Conviction-based action that functions as teleportation under strict physical feasibility constraints. Upon commitment, the World treats the attempt as final.

Upon Conviction, the World evaluates the action for ***Physical Possibility***, defined as whether the Body could complete it under ideal physical conditions using only the Body and what is recognized as belonging to it at Conviction. Physical Possibility is evaluated first without regard to Endurance; only after a complete trajectory is confirmed does Endurance determine success or failure.

If the action isn't within Physical Possibility, Blur produces no effect.  
If the action is within Physical Possibility, Blur resolves immediately as either success or failure.

The distinction between success and failure is determined solely by Endurance. If the required exertion exceeds the user’s Endurance, the attempt fails. Otherwise, it succeeds.

The Body either appears at the realized endpoint or the attempt terminates without execution; no displacement, transition state, or intermediate positioning occurs.

A high-speed reconciliation trace is visually rendered along the evaluated route. This trace is purely visual and has no physical or magical effect.

Any active Dominion does not propagate, persist, or follow the Body during Blur. The resolved action is evaluated solely based on conditions at the moment of Conviction.


## Core Principle

Blur resolves causally at Conviction and is an all-or-nothing resolution. Partial relocation, incomplete manifestation, or intermediate positioning cannot occur.

Blur reconciles the full space of physically possible executions of a non-sentient model of the Body outside of time and causality. 
If a complete execution exists, the Body is realized at the resolved endpoint. The user does not retain awareness or agency during this process, though a coherent sense of the resolved execution will persist afterward.

The outcome of the attempt is determined immediately, based on whether a complete, physically valid trajectory is available for the Body within Physical Possibility, and whether the user’s Endurance is sufficient to afford it. The Body appears at the realized endpoint, or the attempt fails.

The World subsequently renders a high-speed (about as fast as a bullet) reconciliation trace along the evaluated Viable Path. This trace is a visual artifact only and has no physical or magical effect.

_Note: A viable continuation under Path does not imply that the same outcome is reachable through Blur. Path cannot ever predict if a Blur Conviction will work._


## Engagement

Blur requires:  
Primary axis: Conviction  
Secondary axis: Physical action (taking a step)

Physical locomotion is not required. Blur can be engaged while the Body remains motionless, restrained, or unable to initiate movement. In such cases, Conviction alone carries the engagement.

Initiating physical movement is not necessary, but it reduces the Conviction burden by providing embodied momentum. When movement is absent, Conviction must be sustained longer and more completely to substitute for that momentum.
Initiating a step coincides with Conviction, causing arrival to be experienced as the completion of the step rather than as a separate event. Once committed, Blur cannot be interrupted, altered, or aborted.

**Unanchored Engagement Cost**
When Blur is engaged without initiating locomotion, the World resolves the action as a fully committed execution without embodied momentum. The resolved trajectory is unchanged, but the exertion model is denser.

As a result, Endurance is realized more rapidly than in Blur engagements initiated through movement, despite resolving the same endpoint. The realized cost is comparable to sustaining a fast walk rather than a slow one.


## Resolution

An attempted Blur is evaluated solely in terms of Physical Possibility; only attempts for which a complete trajectory exists can proceed to resolution.

Only factors that directly limit physical exertion are considered: strength, Endurance, mass, injury, gravity, resistance, oxygen availability, and substances present in the Body, including chemicals, that would directly incapacitate or kill the Body during exertion.

Any outcome that would prevent the Body from sustaining the resolved exertion mode for the full implied trajectory causes Blur to fail rather than complete resolution.

Blur resolves as though the Body had sustained a single, continuous mode of exertion representing everything it could theoretically accomplish under ideal conditions, including actions the user does not consciously know how to perform. 

This evaluation is limited to what the Body’s structure could physically execute; actions requiring trained coordination, procedural knowledge, or neural conditioning the Body does not possess are not realizable.

The user need not consciously know the resolved trajectory, or exact destination; feasibility is evaluated using the World’s complete structural knowledge rather than the user’s awareness or planning.

**Successful Resolution**

* Blur resolves successfully.
* The Body appears at the resolved endpoint.
* **Physical exhaustion** may occur.

Only the final exertion state is realized as physical exhaustion. No intermediate states, injuries, or transient conditions carry over.


## Resolution Constraints

Skills, techniques, and procedural knowledge are fully evaluated during Blur, but no understanding, training, or muscle memory is gained outside of Blur.

No learning, negotiation, improvisation, or decision-making occurs once Conviction is established.
No procedural, technical, or trainable knowledge is retained.

Despite these constraints, an attempted Blur results in a realized coarse understanding of the action taken and the condition under which it failed, without conferring skill, technique, or actionable knowledge.

Tools, objects, or beings recognized as belonging to the user at the moment of Conviction may be incorporated into the resolved trajectory as part of the committed action. 
Recognition of belonging is determined by direct physical contact or inclusion within the user’s immediate causal extension at the moment of Conviction for the purpose of resolution. This Recognition does not expand, persist, or update during Blur.

Dominion, if active prior to Blur, applies only as the passive state present at Conviction and cannot be actively exerted during Blur; upon resolution, Dominion immediately resumes at its prior extent. 
When Blur begins, all space within the active Dominion field is considered part of the user’s immediate causal extension for the purpose of Conviction.

Environmental objects or beings not recognized at Conviction may still be interacted with as part of the resolved action, but cannot be newly incorporated as tools, resources, or carried objects during Blur. Blur cannot alter its committed trajectory to acquire or retrieve an unrecognized resource.

Carried objects or beings may be manipulated, wielded, moved, or otherwise incorporated into the resolved action insofar as the Body’s committed trajectory requires. They do not independently act, invoke Magic, make decisions, or advance internal processes during Blur.

Passive magical properties affecting mass, resistance, or structural integrity are accounted for. 
Active magical effects do not operate during Blur.

The resolved trajectory minimizes combined exertion and time, not danger.

**Continuous Exertion Model**  
Blur resolves using a single, uninterrupted exertion model. 

Endurance is evaluated as the Body’s ability to sustain a minimum sustained level of physical work for the full implied trajectory.
This minimum exertion corresponds roughly to the effort of sustained walking under load. 
The Body must maintain this baseline without interruption; slowing below it constitutes failure.

Short trajectories incur minimal fatigue. 
Longer trajectories accumulate exhaustion proportionally. 
Blur fails when the Body can no longer sustain the required sustained exertion.


## World Interaction

Blur does not alter the World to enable resolution.  
Obstacles are not damaged.  
Locks are not opened unless they could be manipulated by the Body using only tools already recognized as the user’s at Conviction.
 
Structures remain intact.  
No environmental damage results from Blur.  
No Magical Mediation is invoked to assist movement or interaction.

During Blur Conviction, active exertion of Dominion is suspended, while its passive effect persists unchanged.

Blur applies only to the Body and what it possesses. 
Blur does not propagate outward, leave persistent changes, or modify the environment to accommodate the resolved trajectory.


## Limits and Failure

Physical limits always apply. Blur resolves all outcomes fully at initiation and never partially executes.

**Exertion Failure**
If a complete, physically valid trajectory exists but the resolved exertion requirement exceeds the Body’s capacity to sustain the required exertion mode:

* Blur fails.
* ***Snapback*** occurs.
* Severe exhaustion is realized.

Severe exhaustion is defined as a state in which the Body cannot remain standing or continue purposeful movement.

**Lethal Failure**
If a complete, physically valid trajectory exists, but sustaining the resolved exertion mode would result in loss of bodily viability (e.g., oxygen deprivation, ***Structural Failure***, poisoning) before Endurance failure:

* Blur fails.
* Snapback occurs.
* Acute resolution strain occurs.

Acute resolution strain is realized as a forced suspension of voluntary movement accompanied by brief spatial disorientation, leaving the Body frozen in place for several seconds.
Awareness is preserved, but voluntary thought and action are briefly halted.
Blur resolves this failure state in a controlled manner. Loss of consciousness and uncontrolled physical Collapse do not occur.

**Impossibility Failure**
If the committed action cannot be resolved under ideal conditions with unlimited physical exertion, because no endpoint state exists in which the Body can remain physically viable upon arrival (an endpoint that constitutes immediate loss of bodily viability does not qualify as resolvable), using only the Body and what is recognized as belonging to the user at initiation:

* Blur does not resolve.
* Impossibility failure occurs.

Impossibility failure manifests as a sudden, sharp sensation comparable to an ice-cream headache, lasting several seconds, with no residual pain or injury.

No exhaustion occurs beyond baseline. No injury occurs, and no resolved trajectory is partially expressed.

Repeated attempts within a short interval increase the severity of impossibility failure and eventually results in temporary suppression of Blur responses.


**Blur Example (Situational, Non-Normative)**

_The Body is confined within a locked cell. The door is intact, reinforced, and cannot be forced open through physical exertion alone._  
_The Body commits to escape and attempts Blur. The attempt fails. No displacement occurs. Impossibility failure manifests, indicating that no complete, physically viable endpoint exists using only the Body as it is._  
_A set of lockpicks rests on a table within reach. The Body retrieves them. The Body does not know how to use lockpicks and has no conscious understanding of lock mechanisms._

_The Body commits again and attempts Blur._  
_Blur resolves successfully._  
_The Body appears outside the cell. The door remains intact and locked. No damage, disturbance, or intermediate interaction is realized. No sequence of actions occurred within the World. Only the endpoint state is instantiated._  
_The Body retains no procedural knowledge, technique, or understanding of how escape was possible. It possesses only the coarse awareness that a complete, physically viable endpoint existed using the Body and the tools it possessed at the moment of Conviction._  
_This outcome does not imply that the Body can now pick locks, that future attempts will succeed, or that similar enclosures are viable. It reflects only that, under ideal physical conditions, a complete trajectory existed by which the Body could arrive outside the cell while remaining physically viable._


# Secondary Effect: Contract / Permission

Conceptual-Level Access and Negotiated Causality


## Core Principle  

Permission-Based Causal Access

For entities that have been Indexed but cannot be Integrated, interaction with the World’s deeper causal layers (e.g., fate, time, enforced resolution) requires explicit Permission.  
The World recognizes the Body as a stabilized causal entity, but cannot overwrite, rewind, bind, or enforce fate upon the Body without consent while Indexed. As a result, resolutions that would normally occur automatically instead enter negotiation.  
Contracts do not grant abilities. They grant the World limited, scoped causal access it would otherwise lack.

**Flow Summary:**  
Attempt → Pause → Choice (Accept / Refuse) → World Handling (Normal Resolution / Convergence / ***Terminal Resolution***) → Realized Outcome (Success / Failure)


## Trigger Conditions

World-Initiated Resolution Attempts

*Note: A Contract does not initiate change; it marks the moment when change is unavoidable and overwrite would otherwise occur.*

A Contract state is invoked only when the World enters a form of resolution that would overwrite the Body’s state.

This includes, but is not limited to:
* Rewinding time
* Enforcing loops or resets
* Applying prophecy or fate bindings
* Suspending, rewriting, or overriding causality

The Body cannot initiate these events.  
Contracts arise solely in response to World-initiated resolution attempts.


## Resolution Pause

Negotiation State

When a Contract state is invoked, reality enters a resolution pause relative to the Body.  
Causal progression affecting the Body is temporarily suspended; no outcome advances, rewinds, or is realized during this interval.

Subjectively, the World appears frozen.

This pause exists to prevent irreversible overwrite while Permission is being determined. It is not a simulation of outcomes, but a suspension of Causal Resolution. No capacity is restored or compensated during this evaluation; ***Intrinsic State*** serves only as a reference frame for Recognition and commitment.

During a Contract, the World enters a Recognition-only negotiation state relative to the Body. In this state, Causal Resolution involving the Body is suspended to prevent overwrite in the absence of Permission. The Body remains sentient and capable of Choice, but no physical, magical, or Dominion-based interaction will occur. All interaction occurs at the level of Recognition and alignment rather than execution.

During the Contract state, any evaluation of Choice is performed relative to the Body’s Intrinsic State, rather than its current constrained, suppressed, or externally modified condition.  
During the Contract state, the user can move and observe within the suspended World. All such motion produces no retained physical, informational, or causal change; upon resolution, the Body reverts to its original state and position.  
During the Contract state, interactive presence is constrained to a bounded space defined by the World. The user cannot move beyond this space, alter perspective, or access information outside what is presented.


## Permission and Refusal

The World presents a Choice; Refusal constitutes a Choice, while absence of Choice does not. In the absence of Choice, Forced Assimilation occurs.

* **Accept**: The Body participates under a resolved causal alignment with the World, according to the selected terms if multiple are offered.
* **Refuse**: The World resolves causality around the Body instead, and outcomes are realized without its participation.

Permission is required for any effect that would overwrite the Body’s state.  
Refusal is always possible at the moment of Choice. No Contract can override present refusal.

If accepted, Contractual effects are experientially distinct from normal use of Path, Blur, or Dominion. Fate-level resolutions produce realized effects that are perceived as categorically different in weight, clarity, and pressure.

Acceptance authorizes participation; it does not guarantee success or favorable outcome.  
Refusal may take the form of rejecting the framing or premises of a proposed resolution. Such refusal constitutes valid Choice and does not imply indecision. The World may proceed by reformulating the proposal, including presenting alternate terms, or by resolving without the Body.

**Incapacity Clause**  
Inability to comply due to injury, unconsciousness, or external constraint does not constitute refusal. Escalation requires conscious rejection, not failure of action.

During a Contract state, the World engages the Body through interactive presence sufficient to permit informed Choice, independent of the Body’s physical condition.


## Contract Enforcement

Scoped Coercion

A Contract does restrict future Choices, but it can never override present refusal.  
Acceptance authorizes coercion only to the degree explicitly required to fulfill the Contracted causal outcome.

If accepted, the World resolves a reconfiguration of the expressions through which the Body already moves.

Compliance is primarily achieved through Path, provided the required outcome falls strictly within the scope of the Contract.  
A Viable Path revealed under Contract is unusually coherent and may explicitly designate the required use of Blur or Dominion, which is otherwise impossible.


### Contractual Enforcement on Core Expressions
  
Prior to Convergence, the World can apply limited accommodation to prevent trivial failure while the Body remains the acting agent.

It prevents marginal exhaustion, misalignment, or timing failure from prematurely derailing a Contracted outcome that remains physically feasible.

If expansion of Dominion is Contractually required, the World will temporarily suspend its resistance, allowing extension beyond normal limits. This suspension ends soon after fulfillment or failure.

If the user continues to refuse participation after accepting, constrained Fate Intent manifests as recognitional pressure, intensifying Feedback, narrowing Path availability toward Contract-Compatible continuations, and limiting viable deviation. 

If refusal persists, the World will authorize Blur under the accepted Contract to realize repositioning consistent with the Contracted outcome.

## Contract Outcomes

Contracts persist until their governing resolution either stabilizes or reaches irreversible breakdown.

Outcomes are evaluated based on good-faith participation, not success.

Possible outcomes are:

**1. Stabilized Resolution**

* No escalation occurs.
* No hostility is introduced.
* Mutual Recognition remains intact.

**2. Resolution Breakdown (Escalation Considered)**

The resolution process enters a catastrophic breakdown that cannot be stabilized through ordinary means.

* Convergence may be invoked to preserve coherence and force closure.
* Escalation does not imply fault, refusal, or wrongdoing.

**3. Contract Conclusion Without Stabilization**

The resolution concludes without fulfillment of the Contracted outcome, but without breach.

* Choice was exercised in good faith.
* No hostility results.
* A use of Causal Standing is rewarded.

**4. Contract Invalidation (Forced Assimilation)**
The user knowingly rejects fulfillment after accepting coercive authority, or knowingly fails to make a demanded Choice.

* Reciprocal resolution becomes impossible.
* Causal Standing is invalidated.
* The World proceeds to Forced Assimilation without further negotiation.


### Causal Standing Reward

Through good-faith participation in World resolutions, most commonly fate-related contracts, the Body will be granted Causal Standing.

Each instance of Causal Standing may be expended to permit a single, bounded temporal rewind.

This rewind is not an act of mercy, correction, or optimization. It is a procedural allowance that enables continued participation without invoking catastrophic escalation.

When invoked, the rewind restores the Body to a prior resolved state within a limited temporal window, typically ranging from approximately one hour to one day prior to activation.

The rewind preserves:
* Full Experiential Continuity (memory, awareness, conviction)
* Coherent Self-Identity
* The Body’s physical state as it existed at the selected rewind point

During the rewound interval, the World temporarily relaxes resolution resistance, resulting in enhanced expression handling:
* Path may resolve as direct guidance rather than passive viability sensing.
* Blur ignores Endurance cost for displacements of up to one mile.
* Dominion expansion limits are doubled.

These enhancements persist only for the duration of the rewound interval and imply no further guarantees.


### Causal Standing Constraints

Causal Standing represents unresolved causal closure, not favor, progress, or evaluation by the World. While it does function as a reward in realized outcome, it is never issued as one.

Causal Standing applies only while reciprocal resolution with the Body remains possible. Breach, refusal at demanded Choice, or invalidation of accepted coercive authority immediately negate Standing and permit Integration without further negotiation. Deliberate interference with World resolutions following refusal will invalidate existing Standing and may bar future accrual for the affected resolution context, without constituting hostility or Forced Assimilation.

Delays, resistance, inability, misjudgment, or catastrophic failure do not constitute invalidation.


## Convergence

Convergence

Convergence is a World-executed resolution-layer exposure state.
It is not spontaneous and cannot be invoked arbitrarily.

Convergence may be entered through one of two distinct pathways, determined after a fate alignment check.

**Fate Alignment Check**

* If Terminal Resolution or causal destabilization proceeds in explicit alignment with fate, Convergence is prohibited.
* If the active resolution is not Fate-aligned, proceed to the applicable pathway below.

**Pathway A — Contradiction Escalation (Mandatory)**

Convergence becomes mandatory when **both** of the **two** following conditions are met:

1. Choice Violation After Participation
* The Body has engaged in active participation under constraint—such as coercion, captivity, enforced compliance, or overwhelming asymmetric power—and Choice is subsequently removed or rendered structurally ineffective despite that participation.
* Choice is considered structurally ineffective when all available actions are confined within a sealed Terminal Resolution imposed by external structural constraint that the Indexed Body cannot prevent, and no procedural freedom remains through which the Indexed Body can meaningfully influence outcomes via justification, negotiation, or rule-based interaction
  *  The presence of procedural freedom—defined as the ability to influence outcomes through enforceable justification, negotiation, or structured rule-based interaction—prevents Choice from being considered structurally ineffective, even under constraint.
* Intrinsic biological limitation alone does not constitute structural ineffectiveness. 
* This removal of Choice invalidates the participatory basis of the active resolution.

2. Resolution Failure
* The active causal resolution involving the Indexed Body becomes irreconcilably destabilized and cannot be stabilized through Deferral, Contract-mediated handling, or any available procedural mechanism (this includes ordinary negotiation).
  * Procedural freedom requires at least one responsive mechanism through which the Indexed Body can attempt to influence outcomes. 
* A resolution is considered to be irreconcilably destabilized when continuation can only be achieved by nullifying the conditions that sustain Indexing itself, including the removal of Choice, regardless of whether biological function is preserved.

When these conditions are satisfied, escalation is procedural and required.

**Pathway B — Voluntary Fate Correction (Consensual)**

This pathway does not require the conditions defined under Pathway A.

Convergence may also be entered when:
* World-level causal structures are destabilized in a manner not aligned with Fate
* Direct resolution-layer exposure is required for correction
* The World requests participation
* The Body consents (this is seperate from contract consent)

*Note: The Indexed Body is not foundational to the World’s continued causal operation.*


**Scope and Nature of Convergence**

* Convergence is not triggered by injury, danger, or impending death.
* Convergence occurs only when closure would otherwise require overwrite, enforced resolution, or loss of participatory agency in violation of Choice, or through voluntary fate correction as defined above.
* Participation is investment. When that investment is invalidated by the removal of Choice, escalation is procedural, not retaliatory.
* Convergence is not a power the Body possesses. It is an escalation the World performs in which the Body participates as a causal reference.

**During Convergence**

* Agency is constrained only to the extent strictly required to finalize resolution.
* All recognitional states, permissions, and governing physical constraints affecting the Body’s expressions are simultaneously resolved.
* The Body participates as a causal reference in direct exposure to World-level resolution processes.
* Path operates as sustained, high-resolution alignment, perceived as Path guidance resolved through Blur-level executability rather than advisory guidance.
* Feedback manifests as Fate Intent, an overwhelming recognitional pressure in which all viable continuations are resolved as closed. Exposure commonly manifests as hopelessness or terminal clarity.
  * Less resilient entities withdraw or panic. More resilient entities can exhibit heightened focus, fervor, or uncompromising commitment, remaining fully functional while unable to escape awareness of closure.
  * Fate-sensitive entities perceive this pressure with heightened intensity.
* Blur’s effects are realized without exhaustion and without normal limits on recognized possession.
* Dominion can be expanded or sustained without resistance for the duration required.
* Jinx prevents any entity whose magical systems remain causally opposed to the World’s enforced resolution from activating any non-passive magical ability for as long as the World refuses to clean the damage.
  * For entities that are causally aligned, Jinx is suspended for all affected magical systems.
  * Suspension defers all Incompatibility effects without cleansing, repair, or acceleration.
  * When Convergence ends, Jinx resumes normal decay behavior from its prior state.
* Choice is preserved wherever possible; decision authority is suspended only to the extent strictly required for execution.
  * Convergence operates at the minimum viable level of Choice; further suppression would invalidate the resolution.

Convergence remains in effect only for the duration required to restore coherence or finalize closure and terminates immediately upon realized success or realized failure.

Convergence does not guarantee survival, success, or favorable outcome.


## Contract Variant: Indexed Partnership

Causal Alignment

Under certain Contracts, the World permits Indexed Partnership, establishing ***World-Mediated Recognition*** between two Indexed entities for specific resolutions.

Through this state, the World resolves their causal handling as a single coherent system for the duration and scope of the Contract. This alignment does not merge identity, agency, or Choice. Each entity remains a distinct causal construct with separate Coherent Self-Identity and non-delegable Choice.

The partnership exists solely to preserve coordinated participation in a Contracted outcome and to support sustained observation and coordinated causal resolution where isolated coherence would otherwise fail.

**Effects**

* **Path**: The user’s Path now also has a silvery thread or directional linkage emanating from the user, which only it and the partner can see. This perception conveys engagement and direction only and does not reveal evaluation, alternatives, or Intent.
* **Direct Contractual Awareness**: Outward Feedback is suspended. Direct, bi-directional awareness of the partner’s thoughts, Intent, and emotional state occurs during shared resolution. This awareness is explicit and propositional, degrades sharply with causal distance, and its loss serves as the primary indicator that Deferral range is being exceeded.
* **Experiential Record (ER)**: Feedback’s ER can be engaged by an Indexed Partner, either jointly with the Body or independently. ER access is reciprocal and limited to memories each party is presently willing to disclose without hesitation or internal resistance. Any memory requiring restraint, filtering, or emotional defense remains inaccessible. Expanded access arises only through deepening mutual trust and cannot be forced or accumulated through repeated use.
* **Somatic Ledger (SL)**: Feedback's SL access through inward Feedback can be shared with an Indexed Partner through sufficient Conviction, reflecting mutual acceptance of unfiltered bodily state. This sharing is limited to present-condition perception only and conveys no experiential memory, interpretation, prognosis, or procedural guidance. 
* **Blur**: While partnered recognition is active, the partner accompanies the user during Blur realization *provided physical contact is maintained at commitment*. The partner is resolved as a recognized co-present causal construct, sharing the realized trajectory, but does not initiate, redirect, or modify Blur resolution.
* **Contracts**: The partner participates in all Contracts affecting the user, but holds no authority over acceptance, refusal, or fulfillment except where the Contract explicitly establishes partnership.
* **Dominion**: While within Partnered Recognition Range, the partner can gain one of two Dominion effects (can switch between them but they remain mutually exclusive).
  * Partner Exclusion: Dominion continues to recognize the partner as outside the field, even when within range, allowing the Body to use Dominion without extending its effects to the partner.
  * Partner Dominion: The partner gains access to Dominion to the same degree as the Body, subject to remaining within Deferral range.
* **Jinx**: If exposed to Dominion, the partner experiences residual Incompatibility as normal. However, while partnered recognition is maintained, the effects of Jinx are suspended for the partner. When partnered recognition ends, Jinx resumes normal decay behavior from the state it would have reached had suspension not occurred. 

**Limits**

* No immunity to Jinx, injury, or failure
* No independent use of Path, or Blur
* No transfer, sharing, or delegation of Choice
* The partnership exists to preserve coordination and observation, not safety
* Path interaction, and memory effects are scoped per Contract

**Partnered Recognition Range**  

World-Mediated Recognition under Indexed Partnership requires sustained mutual awareness and causal relevance. Distance, obstruction, or disengagement that disrupts this context immediately terminates partnered recognition.

**Improved Dominion Field (Partnership Effect)**

Under active linkage, Intent becomes a co-primary axis for expansion. Dominion no longer involuntarily expands in response to physical action alone.

In addition, Dominion expression is elevated by one full operational tier at equivalent exertion. Each state of output resolves as the next higher state without additional strain:

* Skin-bound resolves as sheath extension (~2 ft), without unstable projection
* Sheath extension resolves as a stable field (~4–12 ft)
* Stable field resolves as extended field (~12–30 ft)
* Maximum extension resolves as a stabilized field (~50 ft)

This elevation does not alter the underlying exertion model. Respiratory strain remains the limiting factor, but the World reduces resistance to allow higher-order expression to resolve coherently at the same cost.

Dominion may also be expressed through the linked partner as a secondary origin point. This does not create additional output, but redistributes the same field across multiple anchors. Total extent and intensity remain constrained by the Body’s exertion.

In the absence of Dominion, the exclusion boundary may be deliberately relaxed, permitting brief, bounded exposure to the Magical Medium. This exposure persists only while respiratory function remains partially intact. The field will drop regardless of Intent, but Collapse does not occur unless respiratory failure is reached.

Collapse remains lethal. Deferral defers World-level resolution of damage caused by Magical Medium intrusion only; it does not mitigate oxygen deprivation or respiratory failure. Oxygen loss still triggers Collapse, and Deferral cannot prevent death from natural hypoxia.

This change does not grant additional control over Dominion’s behavior. It reflects altered resolution handling by the World under linkage, not increased authority or survivability of the Body.

**Reinforced Engagement**

Under Indexed Partnership and active Contract, Dominion expression requires both respiratory strain and applied Intent. Breathing remains the primary trigger; Intent functions as a co-primary axis rather than a secondary modifier.

As a result, respiration alone no longer produces erratic outward expression, while combined respiration and Intent produce stronger and more coherent effects without altering Collapse conditions.

The exclusion boundary remains fixed in the absence of applied Intent. Uncontrolled pulses, flares, or respiration-driven expansion do not occur. This constraint reflects default World resolution when directive Intent is absent and does not grant fine control, precision shaping, immunity, or resistance.

Breathing remains the limiting factor.

**Deferral Conditions**

Under Indexed Partnership, Deferral remains in effect only while a valid partnered recognition context is maintained.

While Deferral is active, damage that would ordinarily result in death does not progress or manifest physically.

The partner functions as a secondary causal anchor, sustaining a recognition context in which unresolved states can persist even when the Body’s own causal coherence is degraded.

Loss of the partnered context immediately ends Deferral, at which point all withheld damage resumes ordinary physical progression.

The partner does not mitigate damage, alter magical effects, share Choice, or exert authority over resolution. Their role is limited to sustaining the conditions under which Deferral remains valid.

***Warning**: If Deferral ends while the Magical Medium is actively intruding into the Body, Collapse occurs immediately and Terminal Resolution follows without delay.*

**Ontological Note**
Contracts exist at the conceptual layer, not the medium layer.  
They do not alter what an entity is, but what the World is permitted to do.





















# 3. DOMINION

Mutual Recognition

## Core Principle

Dominion is the outward extension of the Body’s Magically Inert nature, arising from Mutual Recognition between Body and World. It expresses through an internal boundary-failure state, in which the Body cannot fully resolve through the Magical Medium.  
In humans, this state is most often encountered at the edge of breath—when strain prevents the lungs from fully emptying and the Body cannot complete its own release.

Within this region, access to the Magical Medium is excluded, causing events to resolve through ordinary physical causality rather than Magical Mediation. Effects that cross the Dominion boundary resolve according to the medium availability at the point of interaction; partial mediation cannot persist across the boundary.

Dominion is a localized domain centered on the Body in which the absence of a Magical Medium is temporarily enforced outward.  
Dominion exists as a constrained field state once established; active assertion, expansion, or modulation of Dominion requires constant exertion and Intent.  
Dominion does not pause or store magical processes; it removes access to Magical Mediation, forcing immediate physical resolution or failure.  
Dominion does not negate the existence of magic. It does not dispel spells or erase magical laws. Instead, it suppresses the availability of the Magical Medium within its bounds, preventing Magic from resolving as magic.

*Critical Note: Dominion excludes a medium; it does not impose rules, barriers, or Intent.*


## Engagement

Dominion requires:  
Primary axis: Physical action (respiratory disruption)  
Secondary axis: Intent

Respiratory disruption determines whether Dominion propagates outward at all.  
Intent determines whether that propagation stabilizes and closes into a field.  

**Baseline State: Skin-Bound Dominion**

While Indexed and conscious, the Body continuously enforces a skin-bound exclusion of the Magical Medium.
* This state is passive and intrinsic
* It coincides exactly with the surface boundary of the Body and has no radius or volume
* No respiratory strain or Intent is required
* Magical Mediation cannot bind to, persist on, or directly affect the Body in this state
  * Effects targeting the Body fail to resolve through the Magical Medium and instead resolve through ordinary physical causality
* This state does not suppress Magic in surrounding space or in objects touching the Body
* Jinx incompatibility does not occur while Dominion remains at this level
* Skin-bound Dominion does not constitute engagement. It performs no shaping, resistance, or area control

**Examples**
* Magical Weapons: An enchanted weapon can be wielded normally. All enchantments continues to function because the Magic is bound to the weapon, not the Body.
* Magical Constructs: A projectile made of magical force or solidified light may pass through clothing or armor, but when it reaches the Body the sustaining Magic collapses. The construct fails at the point of contact and cannot harm the Body.
* Sustained Environmental Magic: Water magically maintained at an unnatural temperature will lose the sustaining effect where it contacts the Body, reverting to normal physical behavior. A caster could continue reapplying the spell to maintain the effect, but the Magic would dissipate almost immediately at the point of contact, making it extremely inefficient.

**Transitional State: Sheath Extension (≈ 0–2 feet)**

Dominion may extend beyond the skin as an unstable sheath projecting outward from the Body’s surface.

This extension represents a partial outward propagation of the Body’s Magically Inert nature without forming a closed domain. The sheath originates from the skin and projects irregularly outward rather than forming a spherical field.

Within the sheath the Magical Medium is absent. A clear boundary separates Dominion space from the surrounding world.

Sheath extension may arise through either respiratory state or Intent.

Respiratory triggers include disruptions in the normal breathing cycle that prevent smooth resolution of the breath, such as:
* Partial breath retention under stress
* Impact or compression affecting respiration
* Sudden exertion interfering with full exhalation
* Rebound inhalation following forceful exhalation

Intent may also initiate sheath extension. Intent does not need to be deliberate; reflexive Intent arising from the nervous system can produce the same effect. Examples include:
* Reflexive defensive responses
* Startle reactions to sudden stimuli
* Sudden unexpected pain

When the Body is calm, Intent may control the extent of the sheath with fine precision. Under stable conditions the extension typically remains within approximately two feet of the skin.

When the Body becomes agitated or breathing becomes disrupted, respiratory triggers override fine control and the sheath behaves reflexively. The extension may flare unevenly before settling as the Body resolves its breath and tension.

The sheath does not form a closed volume and cannot stabilize into a Dominion field without sustained respiratory disruption combined with Intent.

Sheath extension may occur involuntarily or be engaged deliberately through controlled respiratory strain and Intent. It represents a transient extension of Dominion rather than a stabilized domain.

**Engaged State: Closed Dominion Field (≈ 4-12 feet)**

When respiratory disruption is sustained and Intent is applied, the outward sheath may stabilize through a phase transition into a closed Dominion field.

In this state the irregular extension projecting from the skin collapses into a coherent spherical domain that fully encloses the Body. Within this domain the Magical Medium is absent throughout the enclosed volume.

* The field typically centers on the lungs rather than the skin.
* Maintainable radius commonly ranges from roughly four to twelve feet.
* The surrounding world continuously pushes the field back toward equilibrium.
* Sustaining the field produces continuous respiratory strain and accumulating fatigue.
* Increasing the radius significantly increases strain and reduces stability.

This is the first state in which Dominion functions as a coherent domain rather than an extension of the Body’s surface boundary.
 
**Maximum Extension: Full Expansion (≈ 30 feet)**

With full lung expansion under extreme strain, Dominion may surge outward to its maximum extent.

* This state is unstable and rapidly escalates toward Collapse.
* It cannot be safely sustained.
* Erratic behavior and loss of control are common.
* When this state is realized, the field immediately expands to its full extent of approximately thirty feet.

**Termination and Collapse**

Dominion propagation persists only while respiratory disruption is actively maintained.
* When the Body forcefully evacuates the lungs to full exhalation, the exclusion boundary fails.
* This failure constitutes Collapse, during which the Magical Medium intrudes directly into the Body.
* Collapse renders all Higher Expressions inaccessible and is acutely hazardous.
Short surges that release before full evacuation do not automatically trigger Collapse, but increase cumulative strain and instability.


## Effects

Dominion’s natural and stable form is the Body itself. Any extension beyond the Body encounters resistance and reverts once physical strain ceases.  
It cannot be anchored to objects, detached from the user, or made persistent beyond the space the Body occupies.  
The intensity and extent of Dominion are directly limited by the Body’s ability to sustain the required physical state.

Within Dominion:
* Dominion does not restore or repair.
* Dominion forces all things to resolve only through physical causality, eliminating any structure that depends on Magical Mediation
* Failure within Dominion occurs in two forms:
  * If a system’s structure or stored state depends on Magical Mediation, it fails to persist. This constitutes a break; the system does not recover when Dominion ends
  * If only a system’s function depends on Magical Mediation, the system persists but cannot operate. Function resumes once Dominion is removed
* Technological systems are evaluated according to their dependence on Magical Mediation.
  * Systems whose structure, energy source, or function cannot be resolved through ordinary physical causality fail to persist or operate within Dominion  
  * Systems that possess a fully physically causal structure persist, though any mediated augmentation or function fails
  * Dominion does not distinguish between active, idle, or powered states. Only dependency on Magical Mediation determines whether a system persists or fails
*  Stored states persist only if their storage mechanism resolves through ordinary physical causality
  * If the storage mechanism depends on Magical Mediation, the stored state cannot be sustained and dissipates
* Phased or out-of-phase states are treated as mediation-dependent conditions
  * Within Dominion, such states are forcibly resolved into physically causal configurations
  * Where multiple incompatible states are present, they collapse into a single consistent physical arrangement
  * Resolution preserves physical consistency and does not guarantee the survival or integrity of affected entities
* Magical effects that require ongoing Magical Mediation fail to persist
* Enchantments and other sustained effects, including those affecting perception or memory, degrade as Magical Mediation is removed
  * This leaves only what can exist through ordinary physical causality
* Supernatural causation is reduced to ordinary physical causality
* Coincidence resolves without narrative, prophetic, or fate-based influence, unless explicitly mediated through an active Contract
* Illusions and similar effects function only insofar as they resolve into ordinary sensory effects and remain subject to local physical conditions
* Materials commonly described as Magical rely on ongoing Magical Mediation to maintain their defining properties
  * Within Dominion, such materials cannot persist as Magical substances and resolve into non-magical forms that can exist through ordinary physical causality

Dominion may be passively present at low intensity or briefly expanded through Intent coupled with physical exertion.  
Expansion is difficult, taxing, and strongly resisted by the World, producing increasing physical strain and pushback.  
Blur itself does not modify, extend, or reinforce Dominion.

**Cognitive, Memory, and Curse Effects:**

Mental, perceptual, and memory-based alterations follow the same rule. Structures that require ongoing Magical Mediation cannot persist when the medium is excluded. The result is a physically consistent mental state composed only of information the Body has already encoded.

Curses that encode themselves through memory, perception, or emotional response behave identically. When the sustaining medium is removed, imposed structures fail to persist. This may result in the loss of curse-induced memories or the disappearance of behaviors that were not physically encoded.

Dominion does not restore missing information or repair psychological damage. Any effects that have already resolved into stable physical or neurological changes remain.

*Examples: A magically imposed fear may vanish, while a learned phobia remains. Memories of events that never physically occurred may fail to persist, while real memories that were altered may remain in incomplete or distorted form. A repeated death loop sustained by a curse fails to persist, leaving at most a limited or compressed recollection rather than memory of every iteration.*


## Collapse

Boundary-Failure State

Collapse is the failure of Dominion’s boundary in which enforced Magical Inertness ceases.

Collapse occurs when the Body forcefully exhales to full lung evacuation and maintains that state. In this condition, the exclusion of the Magical Medium fails, causing the Body’s Magical Inertness to momentarily cease.

During Collapse, Dominion ceases and the Magical Medium intrudes into the Body, functioning normally for Magic based interactions.

Collapse exception: Soul Magic. Effects targeting non-physical constructs (e.g., souls) are not erased by Dominion, but cannot interact with, alter, or propagate into the Body due to absolute Incompatibility.

Brief forceful exhalation to the point of compression causes momentary boundary-failure. These micro-Collapses are transient and resolve automatically unless full lung evacuation is sustained. Sustained evacuation produces full Collapse.

Collapse is extremely acutely hazardous. Prolonged full evacuation produces acute distress, disorientation, and involuntary gasping reflexes. Collapse is not a controllable engagement and cannot be safely sustained.

When breathing resumes, Dominion reasserts immediately, often as a brief expansion spike driven by involuntary gasping. During this transition, a short danger window exists for nearby entities before regular function settles.

Upon reassertion, Dominion erases all magical effects acting on the Body itself. Such effects are not suppressed, deferred, or paused. Any Magic process that would persist on the Body is terminated, as the Body lacks any Magical Medium through which such effects could be sustained.

Prolonged exposure to the Magical Medium during Collapse is lethal due to Incompatibility, just as prolonged deprivation of the Magical Medium is lethal to entities for whom it is intrinsic.

*Collapse Exception: Whenever it it said that all Higher Expressions are inaccessible, the temperature expression of Path becomes the sole exception.*


# Secondary Effect: Jinx

Residual Incompatibility

Jinx represents the systemic cost incurred by magical systems when Incompatibility is forcibly enforced, persisting independently of the Body’s Intent or continued presence.

It is not an active field, curse, probability manipulation, or deliberate effect. It is the lingering Incompatibility left when magical systems were forced to resolve without access to the Magical Medium.  
Jinx attaches to objects, beings, and systems that were within Dominion while it was active.


## Nature

Jinx affects only magical processes, tools, and dependencies.  
Mggically Inert beings and systems are unaffected.  
The user is unaffected by Jinx, as no Magical Medium exists through which it could apply.  
For individuals, objects, or systems reliant on magic, Jinx poses reliability risk. Jinx interferes only with the activation of magical processes.  
Passive or already-resolved magical effects remain intact unless they require a new directed activation.


## Manifestation

Within affected entities:

* Magic effects fail to activate or fail to initiate reliably
* Enchantments cannot be activated consistently
* Magic plans fail due to missing activations rather than backlash
* Artifacts fail to respond when invoked
* Spellcasting attempts frequently do not trigger

Effects that successfully activate operate normally.  
Passive or sustained effects, once active, remain active and are unaffected.

All effects become activatable again when Magical Mediation becomes available, subject to recovery and decay constraints.

Failures are indirect, inconsistent, and non-targeted.  
Jinx does not attack, curse, sabotage, or degrade active effects.  
It erodes activation reliability only.


## Active vs Passive Effects

Jinx interferes only with activation events. Effects that are already active, or that resume autonomously without directed activation, are unaffected.

**Active Effect** 
An effect that requires directed Intent or will at the moment it is invoked or resumed. Each use constitutes a distinct activation event.

**Passive Effect**  
An effect that, once activated, continues to operate without directed Intent. Passive effects may be dismissible, exhaustible, or power-dependent, but do not require willful direction to persist.


## Persistence

Jinx persists after Dominion withdraws. Its intensity decays over time, reducing interference with Magic activation rather than repairing affected systems.

Restoration of activation reliability is most rapid immediately following exposure, then slows as residual Incompatibility weakens.

* **≤ 2 minutes after exposure:** activation fails entirely (0%)
* **> 2 minutes after exposure:** negligible activation (\~1%)
* **\~1 hour:** severely impaired (\~25%)
* **\~1 day:** partial reliability (\~50%)
* **\~1 week:** mostly reliable (\~75%)
* **\~1 month:** activation reliability normalized (100%)

Any subsequent exposure **resets Jinx to its initial post-exposure state**, erasing prior recovery.  
Severity does not escalate beyond baseline, but recovery gains are lost.

Jinx cannot be cleansed by Dominion, overridden by magic, or consciously controlled.

*Varient Timing: In settings where this is simply too punishing, keep the same rate of recovery as during the first hour, leading to a total of 4 hours to recover to 100% activation rate.*

## Social Consequences

Jinx hinders allies as readily as enemies.  
Those who remain close to the user, or who repeatedly operate within Dominion, experience repeated resets of reliability.

This commonly results in:

* Increasing operational failure
* Heightened risk for Magic-dependent companions
* Gradual isolation driven by practical necessity rather than hostility


### Limits

Jinx does not spread independently.  
It does not alter mundane causality.  
It does not escalate beyond its initial severity.  
It cannot be directed, shaped, suppressed, or selectively applied by Intent.

**Ontological Note**
Jinx is the cost of enforcing Incompatible rules.  
It is the residual Incompatibility left when Magic systems are forced to resolve without access to the Magical Medium. 


# FINAL SUMMARY

This is not a collection of powers.  
It is a Recognition-based state of being.  
The Body cannot host Magic as a medium.  
Magic applies only when it resolves into independent physical effects.  
Indexing determines causal participation.  
Path is the Body seeing the World as it may be traversed.  
Blur is the World seeing the Body as capable of action.  
Dominion is the field arising from the Body’s Magically Inert nature while the World and Body see each other.  
Secondary expressions are consequences of Recognition, not granted features. Nothing guarantees outcomes.  
Nothing grants omniscience or control.  
Everything resolves through Intent, feasibility, and Compatibility.


## Explicit Non-Goals

This framework does not grant:
* Probability control or luck manipulation
* Fate immunity or narrative protection
* Retroactive correction or undoing of outcomes
* Power scaling through experience or repetition
* Automatic optimization by the World
* Hidden safeguards against failure or death
* Supplementary signaling, advisory signaling, or implicit guidance beyond what is explicitly defined
* Protection, mitigation, recovery, or enhancement beyond what the Body’s physical structure can already sustain

This framework does not attempt to explain, model, or permit mechanisms of translation, return, erasure, or reversal between realities. Entry into a Magic based universe is treated as an external initial condition, not a reproducible or reversible process.

All outcomes resolve through physical feasibility, Recognition, and explicit Permission.  
Limits are deliberate. Failure is meaningful.


## Note: Anomaly and Mutual Recognition

This is the proccess of Integrating Anomalies, specifically the ones where the World’s perception through the Magical Medium fails and Integration is resisted, leading to the discovery of Coherent Self-Identity. In such cases, the World permits itself to be seen through Feedback, enabling reciprocal perception without overwrite. When this perception stabilizes, Mutual Recognition arises, allowing Indexing as the preferred method, maintained by the use of Choice.

This sequence is not discretionary; once Choice becomes the active resolution variable, it is the only remaining path by which the World can preserve causal closure without resolving around Choice.

**1. Normal perception through the Magical Medium**

* The World ordinarily perceives entities through the Magical Medium.
* When such mediation exists, the World can see, resolve, and integrate entities directly.
* No special handling is required.
* This is why the process never arises for ordinary entities.

**2. Failure of medium-based resolution**

* Many Anomalies have no Compatible Magical Medium.
* Because Magical Mediation cannot resolve interaction with them normally, the World cannot immediately complete Integration through its ordinary causal relationship.
* Continued presence within the World gives Integration increasing opportunity to resolve the Anomaly.
* Interaction with the World and its surroundings further establishes causal relationships, increasing the likelihood that Integration can overcome the initial inability to resolve the Anomaly.
* In the absence of Coherent Self-Identity, continued presence and sufficient causal interaction eventually allow Integration to complete.
  * Most Anomalies have never possessed Coherent Self-Identity and therefore bear no such persistent causal mark.
* Integration completes automatically and without negotiation.

**3. Integration overcomes inability unless resisted**
* The World defaults toward Integration as soon as sufficient causal resolution becomes possible.
* When no resistance associated with Coherent Self-Identity is encountered:
  * Integration completes
  * No negotiation occurs
  * No Recognition state is established

**4. Coherent Self-Identity resists Integration**

* Coherent Self-Identity produces/resists Integration, and that resistance reveals its presence.
* Once Coherent Self-Identity is discovered a check is made.
  * If the check uncovered that the Anomaly is a being that can maintain Choice, Integration is not permitted.
* This is not a matter of safety, restraint, or limitation.
* It is a policy: **Choice forbids overwrite**.
* At this point:
   * The World will not escalate to Integration using Forced Assimilation.
   * The World cannot yet establish full perception.
   * Resolution must still occur.

**5. The World’s Resolution Policy**

* To proceed without violating Choice, the World permits itself to be seen through a non-volitional recognitional alignment.
* It establishes recognitional alignment, which gives rise to **Path**.
* Once the Body sees the World, it is seen in return.
* Being seen through this link resolves World recognition of the Body as capable of action, giving rise to **Blur**.
* Seeing and being seen by the World grants no power or authority.

**6. Recognition and Consequent Resolution**

* Through stabilized reciprocal perception, Mutual Recognition arises as a bounded recognitional state that makes possible a unique Causal Resolution that does not override the Body, known as **Indexing**.
* To create it, the World first enhances its non-volitional recognitional alignment into **Feedback**.
* Feedback allows Mutual Recognition to become the basis of Indexing, from which **Dominion** emerges as a consequence.
  * The creation and study of Dominion is the sole reason the World employs this unique, and costly, Causal Resolution. 
* Residual Incompatibility imposed on magical systems forced to resolve without access to the Magical Medium manifests as **Jinx**.
* Once Indexed, the World can then engage the Body through **Contracts**, enabling negotiated causal resolution where overwrite would otherwise occur.

**7. Conditional Continuation**

* Mutual Recognition and Indexing remain in effect only while Choice persists.
* Indexing remains in effect only while Choice continues to provide a causal basis for maintaining the Indexed State.
  * Mutual Recognition may persist after Choice fails, until the World withdraws the recognitional linkage.
* When Choice fails, Dominion collapses because the Indexed State is no longer maintained.
  * Mutual Recognition is not itself invalidated by that failure.
* Without the continuation of Dominion, there is no longer a causal basis for maintaining the Indexed State.
* Mutual Recognition ends when recognitional linkage is withdrawn; this occurs as the removal of Feedback.
* Withdrawal of Feedback terminates Indexing and terminates all remaining Higher Expressions simultaneously.
  * This act reverts the Body's status back to an Anomaly.
* Since the Anomaly can no longer meaningfully exercise Choice, as evaluated by the World:
   * Forced Assimilation proceeeds immediately to overcome any remaining Integration resistance.
   * No hostility is involved.
   * No discretionary exception is made.

**8. An Optional Higher-Order Resolution and Deferral**

* Integration normally proceeds immediately once the Indexed State has failed.
* Restoring the purpose of an Indexed State has causal value where practical, but low-energy solutions available to ordinary entities cannot be used on a Magically Inert Body.
* A higher-order, energy-intensive World resolution could alter the Body's state in a manner that restores the conditions required for Dominion, such as a sufficiently extensive temporal realignment.
  * In such cases, the World may maintain the Indexed State without active benefit while evaluating the cost of the higher-order resolution.
  * Forced Assimilation remains deferred rather than invalidated.
* The World evaluates the causal cost of maintaining this possibility.
  * This cost is substantially reduced if the Body has Causal Standing.

**Resolution Invariants**

* Choice is a functional capacity, not a moral or species-bound trait.
* Coherent Self-Identity is the condition whose discovery causes the World to evaluate whether meaningful Choice can be maintained.
* Indexing preserves the causal relationship required for continued Choice and for the resolution of Magical Inertness through Dominion.
* Integration is the default and eventual resolution.
* Forced Assimilation overcomes the additional resistance to Integration imparted by Coherent Self-Identity, which persists even after death.
* The World is the causal universe itself and does not mis-evaluate Choice.


 
## ONTOLOGY FINAL NOTES

* Path, Blur, and Dominion are expressions of recognitional state, not primary mechanisms. Feedback and Contracts are Recognition bridges, permitted to preserve Choice under constraint. Jinx is a consequence of forced Incompatibility and does not preserve Choice.
* The World must maintain non-sentient structural models of bodies to resolve causality.
* Continued resolution requires incremental mediation through a Compatible medium.
* Where incremental mediation is impossible, resolution proceeds only through reconciliation or suspension
* Recognition reveals existing World processes; it does not create new capabilities
* Absence (of a medium) can only propagate through presence, not independently
* Binary outcomes arise naturally where partial resolution cannot persist
* Dominion is not intrinsically tied to respiration. In entities lacking respiratory systems, it arises through an equivalent internal boundary-failure state appropriate to their physiology rather than through breath control

**Ontology Note — Recognition and Expression**  
_Recognition states_ are primary. Path, Blur, Dominion, and related phenomena are secondary only in the sense that they are expressions of how Recognition resolves under constraint. They are not independent mechanisms and cannot persist or fail independently of Recognition itself.

The Body recognizing the World gives rise to Path. The World recognizing the Body gives rise to Blur. Mutual Recognition gives rise to Dominion.
