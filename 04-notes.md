# 1. Note on the Reverse Case of a Magical Reality Entity Entering a Non-Magical Reality

The reverse condition occurs when an entity or matter originating from a reality containing a Magical Medium enters a reality in which no Magical Medium exists.

The receiving reality does not acquire a Magical Medium merely because foreign matter enters it. The imported entity therefore retains its originating structure and intrinsic properties, but any process requiring the Magical Medium cannot continue through the receiving reality.

Magic does not become physically causal merely because it has entered a non-magical reality. A magical process that requires continued Magical Mediation cannot operate where the required Medium is absent.

If a magical process has already resolved into an independently physical state, that physical state remains subject to the ordinary physical laws of the receiving reality. Its origin as a magical process does not prevent its physical consequences from continuing.

The result therefore depends on whether the imported entity or process requires the Magical Medium for continued existence or operation:

* **Independent physical structure:** persists and interacts normally with the receiving reality.
* **Magical process requiring continued Mediation:** fails when the required Medium is unavailable.
* **Magically sustained state:** ceases or resolves according to whatever physical state remains possible without continued Mediation.
* **Physical consequence of a completed magical process:** remains physical and continues according to the receiving reality's ordinary laws.

A magical entity whose continued existence depends upon Magical Mediation may therefore cease to function or die when that mediation becomes impossible. A magical entity may nevertheless remain physically present if its underlying structure is independently physical and only particular functions or properties require continued Magical Mediation.

A magical barrier, for example, may remain present only while whatever mechanism sustains it remains available. A physical body does not cease to exist merely because one of its magical properties can no longer operate.

Likewise, a physical object produced by a magical process remains physical if it no longer requires Magical Mediation to exist. A magically created quantity of genuinely physical material may therefore continue to behave as ordinary matter in the receiving reality.

The receiving reality does not become magical through exposure to the entity. Its absence of a Magical Medium remains absolute.

This is the reverse cross-reality condition of the primary case. In the primary case, non-magical matter enters a reality where a Magical Medium exists but cannot participate in it. In the reverse case, magical matter enters a reality where the Medium required by its magical processes does not exist.

The two cases are not required to produce symmetrical outcomes. The first establishes an intrinsic Magically Inert condition in matter originating from a reality without a Magical Medium. The second establishes the failure or cessation of magical processes when the Medium required for their operation is absent.

Neither condition grants the receiving reality a new Magical Medium.


# 2. Note on the Effect of Indexed Partnership During Convergence

During Convergence, Deferral allows the Body and its Indexed Partner to continue functioning despite having already sustained otherwise lethal damage. Deferral also becomes active for all entities recognized by the World as causally aligned within the active recognitional context.

When Deferral ends, Dominion reasserts and Causal Resolution closes immediately.

For the Body and any Indexed Partner, all damage remains suspended for as long as partnered recognition is maintained. Any subsequent physical intervention occurs only after closure and succeeds or fails according to physical law.

For entities that were causally aligned but not partnered, all withheld conditions immediately resume ordinary physical progression according to their true physical state when Convergence ends.

Where Indexed Partnership is active, existing direct awareness between partnered entities remains in effect during Deferral, even while physical and causal interaction are suspended. This does not grant additional information or agency beyond what the partnership already permits.

During Convergence, outward Feedback resolves as direct, telepathic awareness across all entities within the active recognitional context, equivalent to or exceeding that permitted under Indexed Partnership. This awareness is not a mechanism, channel, or ability, but a forced recognitional state arising from the removal of recognitional insulation during resolution, and it terminates immediately when Convergence ends.


# 3. Note on Temporal Effects

Time Loop Shenanigans

## a. Temporal Effects

In Worlds where temporal realignment, repetition, or rewind occurs, an Indexed Body can undergo repeated resolution without immediate Integration. During such events, Feedback persists across temporal disruption.

This persistence does not grant control over time, immunity to temporal effects, or reliable recall across iterations. Experiential coherence is reinforced unevenly, manifesting as intuition or familiarity consistent with Feedback rather than explicit memory.

Under sustained or unresolved repetition, Feedback may surface residual experiential traces with increasing clarity as iterations accumulate. These traces do not resolve as memory, sequence, or causation, but as partial stabilization of experiential coherence.

Over time, this may manifest as reduced disorientation, smoother engagement with familiar states, or a sense of fit when repeating actions that remain viable. Such effects do not confer understanding, foresight, or control, and may coexist with fatigue, strain, or fragmentation.

Such indicators are diagnostic of current presence only. It does not preserve sequence, identify cause, predict outcome, or prevent subsequent erasure.

Deliberate probing or directed search of Intent Feedback does not surface additional detail, though a diffuse sense of absence or incompleteness will persist.

When a previously viable continuation has repeatedly resulted in outcomes the Body would experience as unfavorable and World-level resolution has occurred, Path may still present that continuation as viable. However, Vertigo will manifest during enactment, appearing on steps that otherwise seem successful. The severity of Vertigo scales with the degree to which the continuation undermines future viability.

This does not reflect recollection, warning, or evaluation of past events. It reflects only structural erosion of viability. Path does not indicate prior failure; it reflects whether continuation remains possible under the current state.

Integration, once executed, is final and is not reversed by subsequent temporal effects. The World may, however, rewind the Body without Permission solely to restore the capacity to choose. Such restoration does not constitute participation or overwrite.


## b. Temporal Realignment

When temporal realignment occurs, resolution enters a suspended state analogous to negotiation or pause. For a fixed subjective duration—approximately ten minutes—the World rewinds to an earlier causal configuration. The duration of the rewind is independent of the amount of time being reversed.

During this interval, the Body remains present but causally excluded from rewinding structures, forces, and entities. The Body may move normally; however, it cannot influence, impede, or alter any process participating in temporal realignment. Rewinding entities are effectively insubstantial to the Body and vice versa. Non-participating matter remains physically interactive.

At the conclusion of the rewind, causal progression pauses briefly. The Body then re-substantiates at its current location. Only direct spatial overlap is reconciled. Any matter intersecting the Body’s volume is displaced minimally to permit physical consistency; no further accommodation occurs.

As a result, the Body may reappear embedded within structures, confined within solid matter, or otherwise trapped by the resumed configuration of the World. Such outcomes are not prevented or mitigated. Temporal realignment restores coherence, not safety.


### Alternative Variant: Compressed Loop Resolution

An alternative form of temporal realignment may involve multiple repeated causal configurations rather than requiring the Body to experience each loop individually.

The initial temporal realignment remains a rewind of approximately ten minutes relative to the Body's subjective experience. The Body remains causally excluded from the rewinding structures, forces, and entities throughout the realignment, as described above.

Where the temporal effect produces multiple loops, however, the World need not require the Body to subjectively experience each iteration. The World may resolve the repeated iterations and then fast-forward the final loop to the temporal position corresponding to the moment at which the original temporal realignment began.

The Body therefore experiences the entire event as a single approximately ten-minute temporal interruption, regardless of how many iterations are resolved during that interval.

The fast-forward does not constitute accelerated subjective experience. The Body simply does not experience the intervening causal progression. Only the final loop contributes to the restored present configuration.

This allows temporal effects involving repeated loops to resolve without requiring the Body to accumulate direct experiential memory of every iteration. Any residual experiential traces remain subject to Feedback and do not constitute ordinary memory, preserved sequence, or explicit knowledge of prior iterations.

The distinction is therefore between **temporal repetition** and **subjective repetition**. The World may resolve a sequence of repeated causal configurations without requiring the Body to experience each one as a separate passage of time.

At the conclusion of the final loop, causal progression resumes at the temporal position corresponding to the moment at which the original realignment began.

All other conditions of Temporal Realignment remain unchanged. The Body remains excluded from the rewinding structures and cannot influence the realignment. Re-substantiation occurs according to the existing spatial reconciliation rules, and temporal realignment restores coherence rather than safety.



## c. Note on Early Anomalous Persistence

Prior to the identification of Dominion, certain Anomalies exhibited persistent external continuity across temporal realignment. Items physically carried or worn by the Body—most commonly clothing—were resolved as co-present with the Body following rewind, despite not being biologically integrated and despite the absence of Magical Mediation.

This persistence occurred only in entities maintaining Coherent Self-Identity. Anomalies lacking such identity did not retain external structures and were fully re-resolved upon temporal reset.

At the time, this behavior was treated as a boundary case of Blur-related handling. In retrospect, it represented the earliest observable evidence that identity could exert causal coherence beyond the Body itself. The persistence of external structures under temporal disruption indicated the presence of a non-local resolution envelope not accounted for by existing models.

Dominion was later understood as the stabilized expression of this phenomenon under sustained constrained resolution.

Temporal realignment applies the same resolution handling observed in early anomalous persistence, differing only in scale.

**Inatimate Objects**
During a time loop, any non-magical object that has not been Integrated does not move, update, or rewind. The World resolves around it.

**Recognition Artifacts**
Rare artifacts formed through persistent recognition interactions may become causally associated with the Indexed Body. Such artifacts are treated as part of the Body’s recognized system for Blur resolution. Depending on their relationship to the Body at Conviction, they may function as anchors, bridges, or extensions of the Body’s causal reference.

**Recognition Artifacts (Experiential Layer)**
Rarely, events that permanently alter the Body’s structure while carrying strong experiential significance may stabilize a fragment of causal memory within that structure. These imprints behave similarly to Experiential Recognition but are anchored to the altered structure rather than accessed through Feedback. Such imprints preserve the event as it occurred, including emotional weight, and may present from a neutral or event-anchored perspective rather than the Body’s original point of view. These artifacts do not grant additional information beyond what was present during the event and cannot be invoked or altered through Intent.


# 4. Narrative Variations

## a. Independent Magical Inertness

The Mutual Recognition ontology assumes that Magical Inertness cannot be created within the World and is instead a property originating from entities that originate from another reality lacking a Magical Medium. For narrative purposes, an author may nevertheless use Magical Inertness independently, without including the main framework, or in conjunction with it. Doing so should be understood as a deliberate departure from this underlying assumption.

## b. Propagating Magical Inertness

Rather than having the interness eventually end at some point after merging with magical matter, an author may instead choose to treat the interaction as non-terminal. In such a setting, the incorporated Magically Inert matter may cause the condition to propagate into surrounding matter, or may initiate a process in which Magical Inertness continues to spread after the original material is no longer distinct. This is outside the default interaction model and may be used for narratives in which the gradual loss of Magical Mediation is itself an ongoing world-level process.


# 4. Note on Resolution Variants (Provisional)

In some cases, additional passive constraints or handling differences may be applied to an Indexed Body.  
These do not constitute new expressions, permissions, or protections.  
They represent altered resolution parameters layered onto an already constrained state.

These cases are not stable features of the system.  
They are best understood as **observed deviations in how the World handles constrained resolution**, typically arising under conditions where standard handling does not cleanly apply.

Such variants may affect:

* stability of participation  
* persistence across resolution  
* perceptual clarity (Path, Feedback)  
* distribution of cost (Endurance, Deferral, collapse risk)  

They may improve coherence, degrade it, or produce no meaningful change.  
Failure is not treated as error. These outcomes are not corrected.

These modifications exist to expose the limits of constrained resolution.  
They are not optimizations, rewards, or guarantees, and may increase risk, introduce instability, or accelerate Collapse.

Normally, World-level effects cannot directly apply to the Body.  
These variants do not override that restriction; they reflect **changes in classification**, not direct application.


## Variant 1: Recognition Overlap (Theoretical)

In rare cases, Recognition boundaries may fail to maintain a strict distinction between the Body and an external object.

When this occurs, the object is not treated as possessed, carried, or influenced.  
Instead, it is resolved as **co-referential with the Body at the Recognition layer**, without meeting the conditions required for Indexed extension or ownership.

Under this condition, the World does not distinguish between the Body and the object as separate causal references for the purpose of resolution.

As a result:

* Blur may resolve between the Body and the object without Endurance cost  
* No trajectory is evaluated  
* No intermediate state is realized  

This does not constitute movement.  
The Body is resolved at a location already considered equivalent.

This condition:

* does not reliably persist  
* does not generalize to other objects  
* does not grant agency, control, or identity transfer  
* does not establish Compatibility with the Magical Medium  

This behavior has only been observed under conditions where Recognition appears unstable or improperly resolved.  
It is not considered reproducible and does not form part of standard causal handling.


## Variant 2: Recognition Across Resolution States [Established]

Multiple Magically Inert entities do not interact through a shared Magical Medium.
No shared field, amplification, suppression, or transfer of Inertness occurs.

An Indexed Body can nevertheless establish a recognitional relationship with another Magically Inert entity. Through this relationship, the World may evaluate that entity according to its current resolution state.


### Non-Integrated ↔ Indexed

When a Non-Integrated Inert entity encounters an Indexed Body, the Indexed relationship may cause the World to resolve the interaction relative to the Indexed Body's stabilized causal reference.

Observed tendencies include:

* accelerated routing toward Integration or Indexing
* reduced persistence of unresolved states
* increased likelihood of immediate resolution following disruption

If the Indexed Body enters Collapse while direct contact is maintained, the World may temporarily fail to distinguish the two entities as separate resolution targets.

During this condition:

* Incompatibility handling may not be applied independently
* both entities may be resolved as a single unit for the duration of the failure

The effect ends when Collapse resolves or contact is broken.


### Integrated ↔ Indexed

An Integrated Inert entity has already been resolved into the World's causal structure and is no longer an independent causal participant.

If an Indexed Body establishes the necessary recognitional relationship, the World may re-evaluate the Integrated entity for Coherent Self-Identity and meaningful Choice.

If Coherent Self-Identity and meaningful Choice are established:

* the entity may proceed as a causal participant
* subsequent Indexing becomes possible
* the entity acquires the additional resistance to Integration associated with Coherent Self-Identity

If meaningful Choice is not established:

* the entity remains Integrated
* no persistence of participation is retained

This does not reverse Integration by itself. It reopens causal evaluation of the previously resolved entity.

Once Coherent Self-Identity has been recognized, the additional resistance it imparts persists regardless of subsequent resolution state. If Indexing later fails, Integration therefore proceeds through Forced Assimilation rather than ordinary Integration.

The World does not continuously re-evaluate Integrated entities. An Indexed Body provides the recognitional pathway through which an already-Integrated entity may be evaluated once for the presence of Coherent Self-Identity and meaningful Choice.


### General Notes

These interactions do not produce new Expressions or Permissions.

They do not transfer Indexing, Dominion, or Feedback between entities.

All observed effects arise from the Indexed Body establishing a recognitional relationship through which the World can resolve another Magically Inert entity.

The distinction between Non-Integrated and Integrated entities is therefore one of resolution state:

* **Non-Integrated:** the entity is already unresolved and may be evaluated directly.
* **Integrated:** the entity has already been resolved and may require causal re-evaluation before Choice and Indexing become possible.


## Variant 3: Convergence-Induced Context Shift (Cross-World Transition)

Under Convergence, the Body may abandon the current World and be reassigned to a different compatible reality.

This functions as a full context shift.

The Body does not select a destination.  
It selects only whether to remain or to leave.

If departure is accepted:

* The World assigns a new destination within a magical reality  
* The destination is not predictable or influenced by the Body  
* The prior World is no longer a valid resolution context  

This transition:

* functions as an effective escape from the current resolution  
* may act as a reset of immediate conditions  
* enables continuation in a new system context  

This does not preserve environmental continuity, alliances, or prior conditions.

The Body retains:
* Coherent Self-Identity  
* experiential continuity
* Indexed status

All other context is discarded.

This behavior has been observed only under Convergence conditions and is not considered a stable or controllable mechanism.


# 5. Note on the World (Non-Operative)

 These notes exist to clarify design intent, incentive structure, and ontological constraints.  
They do not introduce new mechanisms, permissions, or exceptions, and should not be read as modifying any resolution rule defined elsewhere.


## a. On Choice as a Structural Constraint

Choice is not protected for moral, ethical, or narrative reasons. It functions as a **load-bearing interface** that prevents all higher-cost resolution paths from collapsing into overwrite.

Without Choice:

* Indexing cannot occur
* Dominion cannot be produced
* Convergence invalidates itself
* All handling reverts to Integration

Choice is therefore the minimum constraint required to keep non-overwrite resolution viable. Its preservation is mechanical, not benevolent.


## b. On Dominion as the Primary Yield

Dominion is not a byproduct or incidental effect.  
It is the **unique phenomenon** that can only be produced through Indexing under preserved Choice.

Non-magical bodies can exist without Dominion.  
Integrated entities can exist without Dominion.  
Only Indexed Bodies produce Dominion, and only while they persist as coherent causal participants.

**Dominion:**

* Is bound to the Body
* Cannot exist independently
* Cannot be transferred
* Ceases immediately on death or overwrite

Accumulation may occur over time, but has no strategic value beyond continued participation.


## c. On Incentives vs Capability

The World may have strong incentive to preserve an Indexed Body, as longer continuation enables greater Dominion production.  
This incentive does not imply expanded capability.

Because the Body is non-magical:

* The World cannot apply effect-level magic to it
* The World cannot heal, regenerate, enhance, or stabilize it directly
* No degree of motivation alters this constraint

Where continuation is extended, it is done through Deferral, Contract-mediated resolution, Convergence, or causal reversal — all of which are costly, constrained, or hazardous.


## d. On Convergence

Convergence is not dangerous because it is powerful.  
It is dangerous because it operates at the **minimum viable level of Choice**.

During Convergence:

* Dominion is not consumed
* Dominion production is accelerated
* The primary failure mode is accidental overwrite through collapse of Choice

For this reason, Convergence must preserve some degree of agency. Full suppression would invalidate the resolution by destroying the very condition that makes Convergence possible.

Convergence is therefore preferred when survivable and avoided when it would terminate the source.


## e. On Time Reversal vs Convergence

Time manipulation and Convergence are not comparable by scale alone.

* Time reversal is costly but safe, operating within native causal machinery
* Convergence is efficient but hazardous, exposing Dominion production directly

The World prefers Convergence when it increases yield without collapsing participation.  
Time reversal is used when Convergence would result in source loss.


## f. On World Non-Agency

The World is not an actor within causality.

It does not:

* Coordinate third parties
* Act through intermediaries
* Queue actions
* Apply delayed intervention
* Manipulate effect-level outcomes

Any such behavior would collapse the distinction between causal authority and embodied agency and is therefore categorically excluded.


## g. On Learning and Long-Term Behavior

Indexing, Integration, and Convergence should be understood as outcomes of long-term structural adaptation rather than intentional design.

The World may refine resolution strategies over extreme timescales, but any such refinement remains bound by:

* Medium compatibility
* The requirement for Choice
* The non-magical nature of the Body

No future optimization bypasses these constraints without redefining what the World is.


# 5. Ontology Applicability

This ontology does not assume that all universes share the same causal structure.

It applies only in worlds where Magical Mediation exists — a causal layer in which events resolve through mechanisms not reducible to physical interaction.

*If a phenomenon can be described through ordinary or theoretically possible physics, it belongs to the Physical layer and this ontology does not interact with it.*

If a universe contains no Magical Medium at all, the ontology does not initialize. Path, Blur, and Dominion do not manifest.

This distinction prevents the ontology from arbitrarily redefining speculative physics as magic. Wormholes, alternate timelines, and other theoretical spacetime phenomena remain physical unless they require non-physical mediation.

The ontology therefore activates only in worlds where a Medium layer is present.
 
 
 # 7. Onotology Layers

**1. Physical layer**

This is the shallowest one and the least interesting on its own.

* Bodies
* Injury
* Death
* Environment
* Range, exhaustion, limits

Dominion doesn’t override this.  
Blur doesn’t negate it.  
Path doesn’t optimize it.

This layer keeps the world hard.

 **2. Medium layer**

This is where most fantasy systems stop.

* Magic
* Infection-as-magic
* Possession
* Fate bindings
* Zombie animus
* Enhancement overlays

Dominion’s exclusion acts *here*.

This is why zombies fall, enchantments collapse, and “wrong” states unravel.

**3. Recognition layer**

This is the first place the ontology becomes unusual.

* Who counts as a participant
* What events are *yours*
* What failures matter
* What successes imprint

Emotions, guilt, responsibility, and meaning live **here**, not in the physical layer.

That’s why killing doesn’t hurt, but abandonment does.

**4. Continuation layer**

This is where Path operates.

* Viability
* Fragility
* Timing
* Irreversibility

This layer doesn’t care about good or bad — only *possible or not*.

That’s why Path can be silent and still meaningful.

**5. Choice layer**

This is the load-bearing layer.

* Not freedom
* Not morality
* Not agency in the narrative sense

Choice is:

>

Everything else rearranges itself to preserve or respond to this.

This is why suicide is catastrophic.  
This is why coerced survival still matters.  
This is why obedience under threat can be the *strongest* assertion of Choice.

**6. World-resolution layer**

This is the deepest, coldest layer.

* Integration
* Indexing
* Contracts
* Convergence

No intent.  
No mercy.  
No balance.

Just closure policies.

This layer explains why the World:

* invests
* withdraws
* escalates
* refuses to fix things it *would like* to fix

**Conclusions**

None of these layers duplicate each other.

Each answers a different question:

* *What happens?* (physical)
* *What can exist?* (medium)
* *What matters?* (recognition)
* *Can this continue?* (path)
* *Is overwrite allowed?* (choice)
* *How does closure occur?* (world)

That’s why new genres (isekai, apocalypse, cosmic horror) slot in without rewrites.  
They just stress **different layers**
